# 🎧 Daily Spoken English · Day 10
**Tuesday, October 20, 2026 · 10–15 分钟 · A2–B1+**

## 🧥 Clothes That Don't Fit → Asking for an Exchange or Refund
**今日双话题：解释衣服为什么不合身 → 询问换货、退款和付款方式**

**Today's goal:** Explain what's wrong, ask about your options, and politely confirm what happens next.

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: You bought a jacket last weekend. You return to the clothing store and speak with a sales assistant.*

**1. You:** Hi, I bought this jacket here last weekend, but it doesn't fit quite right.  
**Assistant:** Oh, sorry to hear that. What's the problem with the fit?

**2. You:** It's a little tight around the shoulders, especially when I move my arms.  
**Assistant:** I see. Did you want to try a size up?

**3. You:** Yeah, that might help. Does this jacket run small?  
**Assistant:** A little, actually. A lot of customers go one size up.

**4. You:** Do you have it in a medium?  
**Assistant:** Let me check. I'm afraid we're out of stock in that size.

**5. You:** Ah, that's too bad. In that case, could I return it instead?  
**Assistant:** Sure, let me check the return details. Do you still have the receipt?

**🔄 Natural transition:** 先描述不合身 → 询问大一码 → 发现缺货 → 自然转向退货，而不是突然切换场景。

**6. You:** Yes, here it is. I've only tried it on at home.  
**Assistant:** Thanks. Are the original tags still attached?

**7. You:** Yep, I haven't taken them off.  
**Assistant:** Great. This purchase is eligible for a return under our store policy.

**8. You:** Good to know. Could I get a refund, or would it have to be store credit?  
**Assistant:** You can get a refund to your original payment method.

**9. You:** Perfect. I paid by card. Do I need to have the same card with me?  
**Assistant:** Not for this return. I can process it using the original transaction.

**10. You:** Thanks. How long does it usually take for the money to show up?  
**Assistant:** That depends on your bank. You should see it after the refund is processed.

**11. You:** Got it. Is there anything else you need from me?  
**Assistant:** No, that's everything. Here's your return confirmation.

**12. You:** Thanks for sorting that out. I appreciate your help.  
**Assistant:** You're welcome! Hope you find a better fit next time.

> 注：对话里的退货条件和退款方式是**虚构商店的示例政策**，不代表所有实体店都必须接受无质量问题的退货。实际购买时应确认具体政策。

## ② Understand the Conversation｜重点理解

**“It doesn't fit quite right.”** — 穿起来不太合身。*quite right* 在否定句里表示“不是特别合适”。也可以说 *These shoes don't fit quite right.*

**“It's tight around the shoulders.”** — 肩膀附近有点紧。*tight* 紧；*loose* 松；*too big / too small* 太大／太小。*This shirt fits well, but the sleeves are too long.*

**“Does this jacket run small?”** — 这件衣服尺码偏小吗？*run small* 表示相对常见尺码偏小；*run large* 表示偏大。*It runs small, so I'd go one size up.*

**“In that case, could I return it instead?”** — 既然这样，我能不能退货？*In that case* 把“缺货”这个新信息自然连接到“退货”这个新决定；*instead* 表示“改为……”。

**“Could I get a refund, or would it have to be store credit?”** — 能退钱吗，还是只能给商店购物额度？*refund* 是退回款项；*store credit* 是可在店内后续消费的额度。两者不一样。

**“How long does it take for the money to show up?”** — 退款多久能到账？*show up* 在这里表示“出现在账户里”。正式一点也可以问 *When should I expect the refund?*

## ④ Reusable Patterns｜三个高频句型

**A. It doesn't fit quite right because…** — 解释哪里不合身。  
*It doesn't fit quite right because the sleeves are too short.* 袖子太短，所以不太合身。

**B. Do you have it in + size/color?** — 询问有没有某个尺码／颜色。  
*Do you have it in a medium?* 有 M 码吗？  
*Do you have it in black?* 有黑色的吗？

**C. Could I get …, or …?** — 礼貌询问可选方案。  
*Could I get a refund, or could I exchange it?* 能退款，还是可以换货？  
*Could I get the money back on my card?* 能退回我的银行卡吗？

## ⑤ Pronunciation｜发音与连读

**1. receipt /rɪˈsiːt/**：字母 **p 不发音**；重音在第二音节。不要读成 *re-see-pt*。

**2. refund 的词性和重音：** 名词 **a REFUND** /ˈriː.fʌnd/，动词 **to reFUND** /ˌriːˈfʌnd/。  
*I'd like a REFUND.* / *Could you reFUND me?*

**自然连读：** *try it on* /traɪ ɪt ɑːn/。快速美式口语中，*it* 的 /t/ 在元音之间常读作轻快的闪音 [ɾ]。

**英美小对照：** 🇺🇸 *fitting room* / 🇬🇧 *changing room*（试衣间）；*receipt*、*refund*、*exchange* 两边都常用。

## ⑥ Conversation Skills｜React → Detail → Follow-up

**Assistant:** We're out of stock in medium.  
**You (React):** Ah, that's too bad.  
**You (Detail):** I was hoping to get a better fit.  
**You (Follow-up):** In that case, could I return it instead?

对方给了一个新信息（缺货），你先回应，再说明为什么这个信息影响你的选择，最后自然提出下一步问题。

## ⑧ Speaking Challenge｜连续角色扮演

**Round 1 — Explaining the problem**  
**Assistant:** Hi there! What can I help you with today?  
你说买了件衣服，但肩膀太紧，想换大一码。

**Round 2 — Responding to new information**  
**Assistant:** I'm sorry, but we're out of stock in that size.  
先回应，再使用 *In that case…* 礼貌询问能否退款。

**Round 3 — Confirming the refund**  
**Assistant:** We can refund you to your original payment method.  
问多久能到账、是否需要小票，然后礼貌结束。
