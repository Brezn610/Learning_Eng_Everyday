# 🎧 Daily Spoken English · Day 22

**Sunday, November 1, 2026**

## 🎤 Preparing a Group Presentation → Giving Feedback Without Sounding Harsh

**今日双话题：和同学准备小组展示 → 发现内容不够清晰，如何礼貌地提出修改建议**

**10–15 分钟｜A2–B2｜自然美式口语**

今天练习一个在大学、实习和工作中都很常见的场景。两位同学准备小组展示，一开始聊进度和分工，后来发现其中一张幻灯片信息太多，于是自然转到如何给出具体、有建设性的反馈。

**Today's goal:** Check progress, give specific feedback, explain your reasoning, and agree on the next step.

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Emma and Noah are preparing a short class presentation. 人物与内容均为虚构。*

### Topic A — Preparing a Group Presentation

**1. Emma:** Hey, how's your part of the presentation coming along?  
**Noah:** Pretty well. I've finished the first draft of my slides.

**2. Emma:** Nice! Do you want me to take a look?  
**Noah:** Yeah, that'd be great. I'm not sure the main point comes across.

**3. Emma:** Let me see. I like the example you used on slide two.  
**Noah:** Thanks. I thought it'd make the idea easier to follow.

**4. Emma:** It does. How long are you planning to spend on this section?  
**Noah:** About three minutes. Is that too long?

**5. Emma:** Not necessarily. We have ten minutes total, so we should leave time for questions.  
**Noah:** Good point. I might need to cut something.

**6. Emma:** Maybe. Slide three feels a little crowded, though.  
**Noah:** Really? Which part is hard to follow?

### 🔄 自然转场

前面一直在聊展示进度、例子和时间安排。Emma 注意到 **slide three feels a little crowded**（第三张幻灯片有点拥挤），Noah 顺势问 **Which part is hard to follow?**（哪部分不好理解？）。因此从“准备展示”自然转到“提出反馈和讨论修改”，不是突然换话题。

### Topic B — Giving Feedback Without Sounding Harsh

**7. Emma:** I think the chart is useful, but there's a lot of text around it.  
**Noah:** I see what you mean. I wanted to explain the numbers.

**8. Emma:** That makes sense. What if you kept the chart and explained the details out loud?  
**Noah:** That could work. Do you think the audience would still get the point?

**9. Emma:** Probably, if you start with one clear takeaway.  
**Noah:** Okay. So something like, “The results improved after the change”?

**10. Emma:** Exactly. Could you also make the labels a little bigger?  
**Noah:** Sure. I hadn't noticed how small they were.

**11. Emma:** The rest looks solid. Want to do a quick run-through tomorrow?  
**Noah:** Definitely. Can you tell me if I'm going over time?

**12. Emma:** Of course. And you can check whether my explanation makes sense, too.  
**Noah:** Deal. I'll send you the updated slides tonight.

### 难句精讲

**How's your part coming along?** — 你负责的那部分进展怎么样？比单纯问 *Is it finished?* 更自然，也不会显得催促。

**I'm not sure the main point comes across.** — 我不确定核心意思有没有传达清楚。*Come across* 在这里表示信息被别人理解、接收到。

**What if you kept the chart and explained the details out loud?** — 要不保留图表，具体内容由你口头解释？*What if + 过去式* 可以用来温和地提出假设性建议，并不表示过去发生的事。

**The rest looks solid.** — 其他部分看起来挺扎实的。*Solid* 在这里是“可靠、不错”，不是物理上的固体。

**注意：** *feedback* 通常是不可数名词。说 **some feedback** 或 **a piece of feedback**，不要说 *a feedback*。

## ③ Reusable Patterns｜三个万能句型

**A. How's … coming along?** — 问进度但不显得催促。  
*How's your report coming along?*（你的报告进展怎么样？）

**B. I like …, but I think …** — 先指出具体优点，再提出清晰建议。  
*I like the design, but I think the text could be bigger.*（我喜欢这个设计，但文字可以再大一点。）

**C. What if we + 过去式 …?** — 温和地提出一个可讨论的方案。  
*What if we moved this example to the end?*（要不我们把这个例子移到最后？）

## ④ Pronunciation｜两个美式口语技巧

**1. Could you → /ˈkʊdʒə/**  
*Could you take a look?* 自然语速中 /d/ 和 /j/ 常融合为 /dʒ/。先清晰读，再逐渐连起来。

**2. Want to → /ˈwɑnə/**  
*Want to do a quick run-through?* 日常美式口语中 *want to* 常弱化得接近 *wanna*；正式写作仍用 *want to*。不要机械地把 *want you to* 换成 *wanna*。

**英美小区别：** 美式校园里常说 *grade a presentation*（给展示评分）；英式语境也常说 *mark a presentation*。今天的 *slides、feedback、presentation* 两边都通用。

## ⑤ Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设同学说：**I'm worried slide three has too much text.**

**React:** *Yeah, I see what you mean.*（我懂你的意思。）  
**Pick up a detail:** *The chart is clear, but the text feels crowded.*（图表很清楚，不过文字太密。）  
**Ask a follow-up:** *What if we moved the details into your speaking notes?*（要不把细节放到演讲备注里？）

合起来：**Yeah, I see what you mean. The chart is clear, but the text feels crowded. What if we moved the details into your speaking notes?**

如果对方说 *But I don't want to leave out the numbers*，可以接：*Fair point. Which numbers are the most important?*（有道理。哪些数字最重要？）

**核心原则：** 不只说“这里不好”，而是说清楚具体问题，再提出能一起讨论的改进办法。

## ⑦ Speaking Challenge｜连续三轮角色扮演

**Round 1 — Check progress**  
**Emma:** *Hey, how's your part of the presentation coming along?*  
说说自己完成了什么、还差什么，并反问 *How about your slides?*

**Round 2 — Give specific feedback**  
**Emma:** *I've finished my slides. Could you take a look at slide three?*  
先指出一个具体优点，再用 *I like…, but I think…* 提出改进建议，最后追问对方怎么看。

**Round 3 — Agree on next steps**  
**Emma:** *Good point. What should we change first?*  
提出一个具体修改方案，安排一次简短排练，并确认谁负责哪件事。

**进阶目标：** 三轮连续说 3–5 分钟，至少主动反问两次，而不是每轮只回答一句。
