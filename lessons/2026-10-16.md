## 💼 Meeting a Recruiter → Understanding the Day-to-Day Role
**今日双话题：招聘会认识招聘人员 → 了解岗位每天具体做什么。**

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: You meet Maya, a recruiter, at a university career fair.*

1. **You:** Hi! I saw your booth and was curious about what your team does.  
   **Maya:** Hi! We develop software for medical equipment.
2. **You:** Oh, interesting. What kinds of roles are you hiring for?  
   **Maya:** We're looking for software engineers and field service engineers.
3. **You:** Field service sounds interesting. What does that role involve?  
   **Maya:** You help customers install, maintain, and troubleshoot our systems.
4. **You:** Got it. Is it mostly hands-on work, or is there some software involved too?  
   **Maya:** A bit of both. You work with hardware and software.
5. **You:** That sounds like a good mix. Do you need previous experience in the industry?  
   **Maya:** It helps, but it's not always required.
6. **You:** That's good to know. I've worked in technical support before.  
   **Maya:** Oh, really? What did you do?
7. **You:** I helped diagnose issues remotely and explained solutions to technicians.  
   **Maya:** That experience could be useful.
8. **You:** Thanks! How much training do new hires get?  
   **Maya:** Quite a bit. We help new employees get up to speed.
9. **You:** Nice. What does a typical day look like?  
   **Maya:** You might visit customers, check systems, or write service reports.
10. **You:** I see. Is traveling a big part of the job?  
    **Maya:** Sometimes, but it depends on the region.
11. **You:** That makes sense. What's the best way to apply?  
    **Maya:** Through our careers page.
12. **You:** Thanks! Would it be okay if I followed up with a few questions later?  
    **Maya:** Absolutely. Feel free to reach out!

**自然转场：** 从“招什么岗位”→“岗位包含什么工作”→“我曾做过技术支持”→“新员工培训及典型一天”。不是强行跳话题，而是顺着上一句的信息追问。

## ② Understand the Conversation｜重点理解

**I was curious about…** = 我想了解一下……，比直接命令对方介绍更柔和。  
*I was curious about your internship opportunities.*

**What does that role involve?** = 这个岗位具体包含哪些工作？  
*Does it involve a lot of travel?*

**That's good to know.** = 了解了，这信息很有用。适合回应对方提供的实用信息。

**What does a typical day look like?** = 平时一天大概是什么样？这里 *look like* 不是问外观，而是问通常的工作安排。

### 三个高频问句

- **What kinds of … are you looking for?** *What kinds of skills are you looking for?*
- **What does … involve?** *What does the training involve?*
- **What does a typical … look like?** *What does a typical week look like?*

## ④ Pronunciation｜发音

**What does** /wʌt dəz/：*does* 常弱读，*What does the job involve?* 重点重读 job 和 involve。

**kind of**：快速美式口语可能弱化为类似 /ˈkaɪnə/（kinda），如 *What kind of work do you do?*；正式写作保留 *kind of*。

**英美区别：** 美式常用 *résumé*，英式常用 *CV*。

## ⑤ Conversation Skill｜接话 + 追问

**Recruiter:** We provide training for new hires.  
**You:** That's good to know. How long does the training usually take?  
**Recruiter:** It depends on the role.  
**You:** I see. What about field service engineers?

先回应（React），再抓住一个细节（Pick up a detail），最后追问（Ask a follow-up）。

## ⑦ Speaking Challenge｜3 轮连续角色扮演

**1. Recruiter:** Hi! What would you like to know about our company?  
用 *I was curious about…* 开场，再问招聘什么岗位。

**2. Recruiter:** We're hiring engineers who work directly with customers.  
用 *What does the role involve?* 追问，并用一句话说明相关技能。

**3. Recruiter:** We provide training, and the work varies from day to day.  
先说 *That's good to know.*，再问典型一天、是否出差，最后礼貌结束。

**Today's takeaway:** *I was curious about… / What does that role involve? / What does a typical day look like? / Could I follow up later?*

**长期目标：** 先练习 3–5 分钟的自然交流，逐渐延长到 10、20、30 分钟。
