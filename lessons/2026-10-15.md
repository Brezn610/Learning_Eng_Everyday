# 🎧 Daily Spoken English · Day 05
**Thursday, October 15, 2026**

## ☕ Weekend Plans → Inviting a Friend & Making Arrangements
**今日双话题：周末有什么计划 → 邀请朋友并敲定时间地点**  
⏱️ 10–15 分钟｜A2–B1+｜自然美式英语

今天练习的不是正式邀请函，而是和同学闲聊时，顺势约对方一起出门。重点：**从不确定的周末计划，聊到真正约定一个时间地点。**

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Two classmates chat after class on Thursday.*

**Topic A — Weekend Plans**

**1. Alex:** Hey, any plans for the weekend?  
**You:** Nothing set in stone. I might go to a museum if the weather's nice.

**2. Alex:** Oh, cool. Which one?  
**You:** Not sure yet. I've been meaning to check out a new exhibition downtown.

**3. Alex:** I love museums, actually.  
**You:** Really? Would you be up for going together?

**Topic B — Invitations & Arrangements**

**4. Alex:** Yeah, I'd be down! Saturday or Sunday?  
**You:** Saturday might be tricky. I've got some work to finish.

**5. Alex:** No worries. What about Sunday afternoon?  
**You:** That works for me.

**6. Alex:** Great. Should we grab coffee before we go?  
**You:** Sure. There's a café right by the museum.

**7. Alex:** Nice. What time works for you?  
**You:** How about two?

**8. Alex:** Could we make it two thirty? I've got something in the morning and don't want to rush.  
**You:** Of course. Two thirty it is.

**9. Alex:** Do we need to book tickets in advance?  
**You:** Good question. I'll check tonight.

**10. Alex:** Perfect. Should we meet at the entrance?  
**You:** Yeah, let's meet out front.

**11. Alex:** I'll send you a message if anything changes.  
**You:** Sounds good. I'll do the same.

**12. Alex:** Looking forward to it!  
**You:** Me too. See you Sunday!

**🔄 自然转场：** “周末可能去博物馆” → 对方说自己也喜欢 → “Would you be up for going together?” → 双方协商日期、时间、地点。转场来自对方的兴趣，而不是突然换话题。

**理解三句难点：**
- **Nothing set in stone.** 还没完全定下来；比 *I don't have a plan* 更自然地表达“有想法，但未确定”。
- **Saturday might be tricky.** 周六可能不太方便。*tricky* 在这里不是“狡猾”，而是“有点难安排”。
- **Two thirty it is.** 那就定在两点半了！很常用的轻松确认方式。

### ⭐ 三个可复用句型

**A. Any plans for …?** — ……有什么安排吗？
- *Any plans for the weekend?*
- *Any plans for tonight?*

**B. Would you be up for + -ing …?** — 你愿意一起……吗？
- *Would you be up for grabbing coffee?*
- *Would you be up for checking it out together?*

**C. What time works for you? / Could we make it …?** — 你几点方便？／我们能改成……吗？
- *What time works for you on Sunday?*
- *Could we make it three instead?*

**注意：** *look forward to* 后面接名词或 **-ing**：*I'm looking forward to seeing you.*，不是 *to see you*。

## ③ Pronunciation｜两处自然语流

**1. Would you → /ˈwʊdʒə/**  
在自然美式口语中，/d/ 和 /j/ 连起来常听起来像 /dʒ/：
*Would you be up for going together?*  
先慢读 /wʊd ju/，再自然连成 /wʊdʒə/，不要为了快而吞掉句意。

**2. What about → /ˈwʌɾəˈbaʊt/**（常见美式口语读法）  
*What about Sunday?* 中的 /t/ 在元音之间常变成轻快的闪音 /ɾ/，类似很轻的 d。

**🇺🇸 / 🇬🇧 小区别：** *I'd be down!* 在美国年轻人之间很常见；在英国也能理解，但 *I'd be up for that!* 更稳妥。*at the weekend* 偏英式，*on the weekend* 偏美式。

## ④ Conversation Skill｜接住对方的兴趣，再发出邀请

**React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up**

A: *I've been wanting to visit that museum.*  
B: *Oh, really? Me too! Would you be up for going together?*  
A: *Sure!*  
B: *Nice. What day works for you?*

今天多练习 **soft invitations（轻松邀请）**：*Would you be up for…?* 比直接命令 *Come with me* 更自然，也给对方拒绝或协商的空间。

如果对方不方便，可以接：*No worries. Maybe another time!*（没关系，下次吧。）

## ⑥ Speaking Challenge｜连续角色扮演

你和同学周末想约出去，请**连续回答**，并尝试主动反问：

**Round 1 — Plans**  
**Alex:** *Hey, any plans for the weekend?*  
用 *I might…* 或 *I've been meaning to…* 回答。

**Round 2 — Invitation**  
**Alex:** *That sounds fun! I've wanted to try that too.*  
用 *Would you be up for…?* 邀请对方一起。

**Round 3 — Arrangements**  
**Alex:** *I'd love to! Saturday morning might be tricky, though.*  
礼貌换个时间，约定见面地点，再问一个实际细节（比如是否需要买票）。
