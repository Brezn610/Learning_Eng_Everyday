# Day 34 — Buying a Used Bike → Negotiating a Fair Price

**今日话题：** 看一辆二手自行车，询问车况并试骑 → 根据实际情况礼貌地商量价格。

**Today's goal:** Ask clear questions about a second-hand bike, describe what you notice, and negotiate without sounding rude or pushy.

## 10–15 Minute Plan

- 3 min — Read the conversation aloud once.
- 4 min — Practice the useful chunks and sentence patterns.
- 2 min — Shadow the pronunciation examples.
- 3–6 min — Complete the role-play without reading the answers.

## Real Conversation｜原创对话

*Situation: Alex is meeting Jordan to look at a used bike advertised online. The conversation starts with the bike's condition and naturally moves to the price.*

### Topic A — Checking the Bike

1. **Jordan:** Hey, are you here to see the bike?  
   **Alex:** Yeah, I saw your listing online. Is it still available?

2. **Jordan:** It is. I've had it for about three years, but I don't use it much anymore.  
   **Alex:** Got it. Why are you selling it?

3. **Jordan:** I bought a different bike for longer rides. This one was mostly for getting around town.  
   **Alex:** Makes sense. Has it had any major repairs?

4. **Jordan:** Nothing major. I replaced the tires last summer and had the brakes checked a few months ago.  
   **Alex:** Good to know. Could I take a closer look?

5. **Jordan:** Of course. There's a small scratch on the frame, but everything else is in pretty good shape.  
   **Alex:** I see it. How about the gears? Do they shift smoothly?

6. **Jordan:** Yeah, they do. Feel free to take it for a spin if you want.  
   **Alex:** Thanks. I'll ride around the block and see how it feels.

7. **Jordan:** How was it?  
   **Alex:** Pretty good overall. The gears feel fine, but the rear brake makes a little noise.

8. **Jordan:** Oh, I hadn't noticed that. It might just need a quick adjustment.  
   **Alex:** No worries. Has it been making that noise for a while?

### Natural Transition｜为什么从车况聊到价格？

Alex 没有突然开始砍价，而是先确认了车况：车架有划痕，后刹车有一点声音。Jordan 也解释了可能只需要简单调整。

这些具体细节自然引出价格讨论：

- Topic A: Is the bike in good condition?
- Topic B: Given its condition, is the asking price fair?

真实交易中，先了解商品、再根据事实讨论价格，比一上来就问“最低多少钱”更自然，也更容易让对方接受。

### Topic B — Negotiating a Fair Price

9. **Alex:** Thanks for explaining. I like the bike, but would you be open to a slightly lower price because of the brake?  
   **Jordan:** I listed it for 180 euros. What did you have in mind?

10. **Alex:** Would you take 150?  
    **Jordan:** That's a bit lower than I was hoping for. I could do 165.

11. **Alex:** How about 160 if you include the bike lock?  
    **Jordan:** Hmm, okay. We can do that if you can pick it up today.

12. **Alex:** That sounds fair. Could you hold it for me until five?  
    **Jordan:** Sure. Just send me a message when you're on your way.

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个可复用句型

### 1. Could I ...?

礼貌地提出请求，适合看商品、试用或确认细节。

- Could I take a closer look?
- Could I try it out?
- Could I check the brakes?

### 2. Would you be open to ...?

礼貌地试探对方是否愿意考虑某个方案。

- Would you be open to a lower price?
- Would you be open to meeting tomorrow?
- Would you be open to including the lock?

### 3. How about ... if ...?

提出一个带条件的折中方案。

- How about 160 if you include the lock?
- How about Friday if that works better for you?
- How about splitting the cost if we both use it?

## Pronunciation｜2 个自然美式发音技巧

### 1. Would you → /wʊdʒə/

在轻松、连续的美式口语里，would you 常融合成一个更顺的音。

Practice:

- Would you take 150?
- Would you be open to that?

重点：不要把两个词读得完全割裂；自然对话里会连在一起。

### 2. A little：注意中间的轻弹音

在美式英语中，little 里的 t 常变成轻弹音 /ɾ/，听起来比清晰爆破的 /t/ 更轻。

Practice:

- a little noise
- a little lower
- come down a little

不要刻意把每个辅音都读得很重，保持轻松的节奏。

### American vs. British English

- **used bike** 在美式英语里很常见；英式英语也常说 **second-hand bike**。
- **Could you come down a little?** 是自然的美式议价表达。
- **Could you knock a little off the price?** 在英式英语里也很常见，意思是“能不能再便宜一点？”

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设卖家说：

**Jordan:** The rear brake makes a little noise, but it might just need a quick adjustment.

不要马上只说 "Okay." 试着分三步接话：

1. **React — 回应**  
   *Thanks for letting me know.*  
   谢谢你告诉我。

2. **Pick up a detail — 抓住细节**  
   *You mentioned the rear brake.*  
   你刚才提到了后刹车。

3. **Ask a follow-up — 顺着细节追问**  
   *Has it been doing that for a while, or did it just start?*  
   这个情况持续一段时间了，还是最近才出现？

连起来练习：

*Thanks for letting me know. You mentioned the rear brake. Has it been doing that for a while, or did it just start?*

**练习原则：** 先回应，再抓一个具体细节，最后问一个能让对方补充信息的问题。这样听起来像真正的交流，而不是机械地问问题。

## Speaking Challenge｜3 个连续角色扮演问题

你是 Alex，我是 Jordan。请用英文回答，每轮尽量说 3–5 句话，并主动追问一个细节。

### Round 1 — Ask About the Bike

**Jordan:** Hi! Are you interested in the bike? It's still available.

你的任务：
- 说明你是在哪里看到广告的。
- 询问车龄、维修记录和出售原因。
- 不要只问一个问题，顺着回答继续追问。

### Round 2 — Check the Condition

**Jordan:** It's in pretty good shape. There are a few scratches, but nothing serious. You can take it for a spin.

你的任务：
- 请求仔细检查车架、轮胎或刹车。
- 说出你实际注意到的一个细节。
- 用 React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up 继续交流。

### Round 3 — Make an Offer

**Jordan:** I'm asking 180 euros, but I'm willing to hear a reasonable offer.

你的任务：
- 礼貌地提出一个具体价格。
- 解释你的报价依据。
- 如果对方还价，尝试提出一个折中方案。
- 最后确认取车时间。
