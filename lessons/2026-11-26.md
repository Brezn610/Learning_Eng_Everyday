# Day 47 — Discussing Salary Expectations → Clarifying Working Hours and Benefits

**今日话题：** 面试中讨论薪资期望 → 进一步确认工作时间、加班和福利安排

**练习时长：** 10–15 分钟 · **目标：** 用简洁自然的美式英语谈薪资期望，不显得生硬；继续追问薪酬结构和工作条件。

## Real Conversation｜原创对话

*Situation: Taylor is interviewing for a junior role. Morgan is the hiring manager. Near the end of the interview, Morgan invites Taylor to ask questions. The conversation moves from salary expectations to the details of the working arrangement.*

### Topic A — Discussing Salary Expectations

**1. Morgan:** Thanks for walking me through your experience. Before we wrap up, do you have any questions about the role?

**Taylor:** Yes, actually. Could you share the salary range budgeted for this position?

**2. Morgan:** Sure. We have a range in mind, but the final offer depends on the candidate's experience and the scope of the role.

**Taylor:** That makes sense. I'd like to understand the range so I can make sure we're on the same page.

**3. Morgan:** Of course. Do you already have a salary expectation?

**Taylor:** I do. Based on the responsibilities and my experience, I'm looking for something in the range we discussed, but I'm open to talking about the overall package.

**4. Morgan:** That's helpful. Just to clarify, when you say that range, are you thinking about your base salary or the total compensation?

**Taylor:** I was thinking about the annual base salary, before taxes. Are bonuses or other benefits included separately?

**5. Morgan:** The base salary is fixed. There's also a performance-based bonus, but it's not guaranteed.

**Taylor:** Got it. How is that bonus usually evaluated? Is it based on individual goals, team results, or both?

**6. Morgan:** It's a mix of individual and team goals. We review performance regularly, and the details are explained when we make an offer.

**Taylor:** Thanks, that's useful to know. I'd also like to understand how the role is structured day to day.

### Topic B — Clarifying Working Hours and Benefits

**7. Morgan:** Sure. What would you like to know?

**Taylor:** What does a typical workweek look like? Are the hours fixed, or is there some flexibility?

**8. Morgan:** The team has core collaboration hours, but people can usually adjust their start and finish times with their manager.

**Taylor:** That's good to hear. How does the team handle overtime when a project gets busy?

**9. Morgan:** It doesn't happen every week, but there can be busy periods. We try to plan ahead and agree on priorities rather than expect people to work late regularly.

**Taylor:** I appreciate that. Is extra time usually paid, or is it handled through time off later?

**10. Morgan:** That depends on the contract and the arrangement for the role. We'd make the policy clear before you accept an offer.

**Taylor:** Understood. And what's the usual office arrangement? Does the team work on-site every day?

**11. Morgan:** The team works in a hybrid setup. We normally meet in person a couple of days a week, and the rest can be worked remotely, depending on the project.

**Taylor:** That sounds reasonable. Are those office days fixed, or does the team decide them together?

**12. Morgan:** The team agrees on them based on meetings and project needs.

**Taylor:** Great. So, just to recap: we'll discuss the base salary and bonus separately, and the offer will spell out the working-hours and overtime arrangements. The team also has a hybrid schedule. Is that right?

**Morgan:** Exactly. If we move forward, we'll put the details in writing so you can review everything before making a decision.

### 自然转场为什么成立？

**Topic A：先谈薪资期望。** 面试官邀请候选人提问，候选人先询问 salary range，再确认 base salary、bonus 和 total compensation 的区别。

**Topic B：从薪酬自然延伸到工作条件。** 当双方谈到 bonus 如何评估时，候选人顺势询问日常工作安排。薪资并不是整个 offer 的全部，因此继续确认 working hours、overtime 和 hybrid setup 很自然。

真实交流中，谈薪资不必一次问完所有问题。先问范围，再抓住对方提到的一个细节继续确认，听起来更像对话，而不是照着清单盘问。

**特别记住：**

- **salary range** 是薪资范围，不是你必须接受的具体数字。
- **base salary** 是基本工资；bonus、benefits 等可能另外计算。
- **before taxes** 表示税前。讨论数字时，确认税前还是税后，避免双方理解不同。
- **I'm open to...** 表示愿意讨论，不等于没有自己的底线。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个高频句型

### 1. Could you share the range for...?

礼貌询问范围：

- Could you share the salary range for this position?
- Could you share the expected working hours?
- Could you share the timeline for the next step?

### 2. I'm looking for..., but I'm open to...

表达期望，同时保留协商空间：

- I'm looking for a competitive salary, but I'm open to discussing the overall package.
- I'm looking for a role with room to grow, but I'm open to different team structures.

不要只说 I'm flexible。先表达自己的考虑，再说愿意讨论什么，会更清楚。

### 3. Just to clarify, does that include...?

确认对方刚刚说的内容：

- Just to clarify, does that include the annual bonus?
- Just to clarify, are those hours fixed?
- Just to clarify, is the role hybrid or fully on-site?

## Pronunciation｜自然美式语流

**1. Could you**

在快速自然的美式英语中，could you 往往连读，you 的开头音会和前面的 d 融合。练习时不要逐词停顿：

- Could you share the range?
- Could you explain the bonus structure?
- Could you clarify the working hours?

**2. I'd like to**

I'd like to 中的 to 通常弱读。整句的重音放在真正重要的信息上：

- I'd like to understand the package.
- I'd like to ask about overtime.
- I'd like to know what happens next.

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设面试官说：

**Morgan:** The bonus is performance-based, and it's a mix of individual and team goals.

**1 · React**

That sounds fair.

听起来挺合理的。

**2 · Pick up a detail**

So both my own performance and the team's results matter.

所以个人表现和团队结果都会影响奖金。

**3 · Ask a follow-up**

How often are those goals reviewed?

这些目标多久评估一次？

再练一个：

**Morgan:** The team has core collaboration hours, but there's some flexibility.

- **React:** That's good to hear.
- **Pick up a detail:** So I don't necessarily have to start at exactly the same time every day.
- **Ask a follow-up:** Are the core hours the same for everyone on the team?

重点：不要马上跳到下一个准备好的问题。先回应，再抓住一个细节，最后问一个能帮助你理解实际工作方式的问题。

## Speaking Challenge｜3 轮连续角色扮演

你扮演 Taylor，我扮演 Morgan（hiring manager）。每轮尽量回答 3–5 句话，并在回答中自然提出一个 follow-up question。

### Round 1 — Salary Expectations

**Morgan:** Before we wrap up, do you have any questions about the role or the salary range?

你的任务：
- 询问这个职位的薪资范围。
- 说明自己对薪资的期望，但不要显得强硬。
- 询问对方说的是税前基本工资还是总薪酬。

### Round 2 — Understanding the Package

**Morgan:** The base salary is separate from the performance-based bonus. The bonus depends on individual and team goals.

你的任务：
- 先回应对方的信息。
- 抓住 performance-based 这个细节。
- 追问奖金如何评估，以及是否有其他福利。

### Round 3 — Working Conditions

**Morgan:** The team works in a hybrid setup, and we have some flexibility around working hours. Is there anything else you'd like to know?

你的任务：
- 询问每周工作时间和 core hours。
- 询问加班如何处理。
- 询问办公室出勤安排是否固定。
- 用 So, just to recap... 总结你理解的 offer 条件。
