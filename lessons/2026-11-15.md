# Day 36 — Picking Up a Rental Car → Checking Existing Damage and Confirming the Coverage

**今日话题：** 在租车柜台确认租车条款 → 取车前检查已有划痕、拍照留证并确认保险责任

**Today's goal:** Ask clear questions about a rental agreement, check the car before driving away, and make sure any existing damage is recorded.

## Real Conversation｜12 轮原创双人对话

*Situation: Sam is picking up a rental car at an airport. Taylor works at the rental desk and then walks Sam to the car.*

### Topic A — At the Rental Desk

**1. Taylor:** Hi! Are you here to pick up a rental car?

**Sam:** Yes. I booked a small car online under my name.

**2. Taylor:** Great. I just need to check your driver's license and booking confirmation. Will anyone else be driving?

**Sam:** Yes, my friend might drive too. Do I need to add them to the agreement?

**3. Taylor:** Yes. Anyone who drives the car needs to be listed as an additional driver. There's a small daily fee.

**Sam:** Okay, good to know. Is there a mileage limit?

**4. Taylor:** No, this booking includes unlimited mileage. The fuel policy is full-to-full, so please bring it back with a full tank.

**Sam:** Got it. So I pick it up full and return it full, right?

**5. Taylor:** Exactly. Now, about the coverage: your booking includes basic coverage, but the deductible is €900.

**Sam:** Just to make sure I understand, I'd have to pay up to €900 if the car gets damaged?

**6. Taylor:** That's the amount you may be responsible for, depending on the damage and the terms. You can choose an extra damage waiver to reduce it.

**Sam:** I see. Could you walk me through what's covered before I decide?

### 自然转场为什么成立？

Sam 已经问清楚了驾驶人、里程、油量政策和保险责任。Taylor 接下来要带 Sam 去停车场取车。

这时，讨论从“合同里写了什么”自然转到“眼前这辆车原本就有什么问题”。如果不在开走前检查并记录，之后就可能难以说明某处划痕是不是原来就有的。

因此 Topic B 是 Topic A 的实际下一步，而不是突然换场景。

### Topic B — Inspecting the Car Before Driving Away

**7. Taylor:** Sure. Let's go over the main points. The extra waiver reduces your financial responsibility, but it doesn't cover every situation.

**Sam:** Okay. I'll read the details. Can we check the car together before I leave?

**8. Taylor:** Of course. There's a small scratch on the rear bumper. It's already noted on the inspection report.

**Sam:** I can see it. Could you show me where it's marked on the report?

**9. Taylor:** Here it is. The scratch is marked on the right side of the rear bumper.

**Sam:** Thanks. I also noticed a tiny chip on the windshield. I don't see that on the report.

**10. Taylor:** Good catch. Let's add it before you drive off. I'll make a note and take a photo.

**Sam:** Could I take a few photos too, just to have a record?

**11. Taylor:** Absolutely. It's a good idea to take photos of all sides of the car, including the wheels and the interior.

**Sam:** I'll do that. And could you confirm the fuel level is recorded as full?

**12. Taylor:** Yes, it's marked as full. Here's your key. Have a safe trip!

**Sam:** Thanks for your help. I'll return it with a full tank and bring it back to this location.

### 重要难句

- **Anyone who drives the car needs to be listed as an additional driver.**  
  任何会开这辆车的人，都需要登记为额外驾驶人。
- **The fuel policy is full-to-full.**  
  油量政策是满油取车、满油还车。
- **You may be responsible for the damage, depending on the terms.**  
  根据合同条款，你可能需要承担相应的损坏费用。
- **Could you show me where it's marked on the report?**  
  你能给我看看报告上具体标记在哪里吗？
- **Good catch. Let's add it before you drive off.**  
  你发现得好。我们在你开走之前把它补充进去。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个可复用高频句型

### 1. Before I ..., could you ...?

在采取行动之前，请对方先解释或确认。

- Before I sign, could you explain the deductible?
- Before I drive off, could we check the car?
- Before I return it, should I take another photo of the fuel gauge?

中文：在我……之前，能不能先……？

### 2. I noticed ... I don't see it on ... 

指出一个自己发现、但记录里似乎没有的细节。

- I noticed a scratch. I don't see it on the report.
- I noticed the fuel level is below full. Is that recorded?
- I noticed a small crack near the mirror. Could you check it?

中文：我注意到……，但我在……上没有看到它。

### 3. Just to make sure I understand, ...?

用自己的话复述对方的信息，确认没有理解错。

- Just to make sure I understand, I need to return it with a full tank?
- Just to make sure I understand, my friend needs to be listed too?
- Just to make sure I understand, the deductible is €900?

中文：我确认一下我的理解……对吗？

## Pronunciation｜2 个自然美式语流技巧

1. **Could you 的融合：** 在自然口语中，could you 常连成更紧凑的音，听起来接近 /kʊdʒə/。练习：*Could you walk me through it?*、*Could you check this?*。重读真正的信息词，例如 WALK、CHECK。
2. **to 的弱读：** 在 *need to add*、*want to check*、*have to return* 中，非重读的 to 通常弱化为 /tə/。不要每个词都用同样的力度；把重点放在 add、check、return 上。

### 美式与英式用法

| 美式英语 | 英式英语 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| car rental / rental car | car hire / hire car | 租车／租来的车 |
| deductible | excess | 保险免赔额／自付额 |
| parking lot | car park | 停车场 |

在欧洲租车时，合同上经常会看到 **excess**；美式英语里更常用 **deductible**。具体保障范围仍以租车合同为准，不要只凭术语判断。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设工作人员说：

**Taylor:** There's a small scratch on the rear bumper. It's already noted on the inspection report.

不要只回答 *Okay.*

试试三步：

1. **React — 先回应：** *Thanks for pointing that out.*  
   谢谢你指出来。
2. **Pick up a detail — 抓住细节：** *You mentioned it's on the rear bumper.*  
   你刚才说它在后保险杠上。
3. **Ask a follow-up — 继续追问：** *Could you show me where it's marked on the report?*  
   你能给我看看报告上具体标记在哪里吗？

连起来练：

*Thanks for pointing that out. You mentioned it's on the rear bumper. Could you show me where it's marked on the report?*

再进阶一步，如果工作人员说已经记录，你可以继续：

*Great. Could we take a photo of it as well, just to be safe?*

这样既回应了对方，也抓住了一个具体细节，并提出了合理的下一步请求。

## Speaking Challenge｜3 个连续角色扮演问题

你扮演 Sam，我扮演租车工作人员 Taylor。每轮尽量说 3–5 句话，并主动提出一个 follow-up question。

### Round 1 — Clarify the Rental Agreement

**Taylor:** Hi! Are you picking up the car today? Will anyone else be driving?

你的任务：
- 确认你有预订。
- 询问是否可以添加另一位驾驶人。
- 确认里程是否有限制，以及油量政策是什么。
- 用 *Just to make sure I understand...* 复述一个条款。

### Round 2 — Ask About the Coverage

**Taylor:** Your booking includes basic coverage, but the deductible is €900. Would you like to hear about the extra coverage?

你的任务：
- 询问 deductible 具体意味着什么。
- 追问额外保障包括和不包括什么。
- 不要急着答应，先请工作人员解释清楚。
- 用自己的话确认理解。

### Round 3 — Inspect the Car

**Taylor:** Here's the car. There's already a small scratch on the rear bumper. It's on the report.

你的任务：
- 回应工作人员。
- 检查车身、轮胎和挡风玻璃。
- 指出一个报告上没有记录的损坏。
- 请工作人员补充记录并拍照。
- 确认油量和还车地点后自然结束对话。
