# Day 32 — Feeling Out of Touch → Setting Up a Regular Catch-up Call

**今日话题：** 忙碌生活中感觉和朋友渐渐失去联系 → 自然商量一个适合双方时区的固定通话时间。

**Today's goal:** Talk honestly about staying in touch, explain why you have been busy, and make a low-pressure plan to catch up.

## Real Conversation｜原创对话

*Situation: Two friends who now live in different countries have a video call after not talking properly for a few weeks.*

**1. Maya:** Hey, it's been a while. How have you been?  
**Leo:** Pretty good, just busy. How about you?

**2. Maya:** Same here. I realized we haven't really caught up since I moved.  
**Leo:** Yeah, our messages have been pretty short lately.

**3. Maya:** I keep meaning to call, but the week gets away from me.  
**Leo:** I get that. What have you been busy with?

**4. Maya:** Mostly classes and trying to settle into a routine. It's going well, but weekends can feel a little quiet.  
**Leo:** Do you ever get homesick?

**5. Maya:** Sometimes. I miss being able to drop by a friend's place without planning ahead.  
**Leo:** Yeah, it's the spontaneous stuff you miss.

**6. Maya:** Exactly. I still text everyone, but it's not quite the same.  
**Leo:** Have you tried setting up a regular call?

**自然转场为什么成立？** 前面两个人在聊搬家后的生活、忙碌和想念朋友。Maya 提到虽然一直发消息，但感觉不太一样；Leo 顺着“联系不够深入”这个细节，提出固定通话的办法。话题因此从“为什么最近没怎么联系”自然转到“怎样安排一次真正能聊起来的电话”。

**7. Maya:** Not really. We usually say, “Let's talk soon,” and then never pick a time.  
**Leo:** Maybe we should make it easier and choose a day.

**8. Maya:** That could work. How about Sunday?  
**Leo:** Sunday might be good. What time works on your end?

**9. Maya:** I'm six hours ahead of you, right? If we do 10 a.m. your time, it'll be 4 p.m. here.  
**Leo:** That's actually perfect. I'm usually free then.

**10. Maya:** Would you rather do a video call or just talk on the phone?  
**Leo:** Video would be nice, but no pressure if one of us is out.

**11. Maya:** Good point. Let's keep it casual. If something comes up, we can just reschedule.  
**Leo:** Sounds good. We don't have to make it a big thing.

**12. Maya:** I'll send you a calendar invite for this Sunday.  
**Leo:** Perfect. It'll be nice to properly catch up.

### 重要难句

- **I keep meaning to call, but the week gets away from me.**  
  我一直想着要打电话，但一周忙着忙着就过去了。这里的 *the week gets away from me* 是很自然的口语说法，不是字面上的“星期逃走了”。

- **I miss being able to drop by a friend's place without planning ahead.**  
  我很怀念不用提前安排、想去朋友家就能顺路过去的感觉。

- **What time works on your end?**  
  你那边什么时间方便？尤其适合跨时区约时间。

- **If something comes up, we can just reschedule.**  
  如果临时有事，我们再改时间就好。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个可复用高频句型

### 1. I keep meaning to ..., but ...

表示“一直想着要做……，但总是没做成”，很适合解释拖延或忙碌。

- I keep meaning to call my parents, but I've been swamped.
- I keep meaning to reply, but things have been hectic.

### 2. What time works on your end?

跨时区、跨日程约时间时非常自然。

- I'm free after six. What time works on your end?
- We could do Saturday morning. Does that work on your end?

### 3. If something comes up, we can ...

提前给计划留出弹性，不会让对方觉得有压力。

- If something comes up, we can move it to next week.
- If you're running late, we can just start a little later.

## Pronunciation｜2 个自然语流技巧

1. **Want to → wanna**：在非常口语、非正式的美式英语中，*want to* 常弱化成 /ˈwɑnə/。例如 *Do you wanna talk on Sunday?* 正式邮件或需要清晰表达时仍用 *want to*。
2. **to 的弱读**：在 *time to talk*、*need to call* 里，非重读的 *to* 通常读成 /tə/，而不是每次都读得很重。把重点放在 *time*、*talk*、*call* 等信息词上。

**美英差别：** *schedule* 美式常读 /ˈskedʒuːl/，英式常读 /ˈʃedjuːl/。两种发音都正确。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

对方说：

**Leo:** Weekends can feel a little quiet when you don't know many people nearby.

不要只回答 *Yeah, I know.* 试着这样接：

1. **React — 先回应：** *I can imagine. It takes time to build a new circle.*  
   我能理解。建立新的朋友圈确实需要时间。
2. **Pick up a detail — 抓住细节：** *So you mostly spend your weekends on your own at the moment?*  
   所以你现在周末大多是一个人过？
3. **Ask a follow-up — 继续追问：** *Have you found any places or activities where it's easy to meet people?*  
   你有没有找到一些比较容易认识新朋友的地方或活动？

如果对方说最近参加了一个语言交换活动，可以继续问：

*How was it? Did you get to talk to many people?*  
感觉怎么样？你有机会和很多人聊天吗？

重点不是连续抛出问题，而是先对对方的感受作出回应，再抓住一个具体细节，让对话顺着对方的回答自然延伸。

## Speaking Challenge｜3 个连续角色扮演问题

你扮演 Maya，我扮演 Leo。每轮尽量说 3–5 句话，并在回答后主动问我一个问题。

### Round 1 — Catching up

**Leo:** Hey! It's been ages. How's life been since you moved?

你的任务：
- 简单描述最近的生活和学习/工作节奏。
- 说一个你喜欢的新变化，以及一个还不太习惯的地方。
- 接着问我最近怎么样。

### Round 2 — Talking about staying in touch

**Leo:** I know what you mean. I sometimes feel like texting isn't quite the same as having a proper conversation.

你的任务：
- 回应这个感受。
- 说说你更喜欢电话、视频还是面对面聊天，以及原因。
- 追问我平时怎样和远方的朋友保持联系。

### Round 3 — Making a plan

**Leo:** How about Sunday? I'm free in the morning, but I know we're in different time zones.

你的任务：
- 先确认双方时差。
- 提出一个具体时间。
- 商量视频还是语音，并说明如果临时有事怎么办。
- 用自然的方式结束对话。
