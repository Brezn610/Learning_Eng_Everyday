# Day 39 — Getting a Coworking Space Day Pass → Booking a Meeting Room for a Video Call

**今日话题：** 询问共享办公空间的日票 → 因为下午有视频会议，自然延伸到预约会议室、确认设备和费用。

**练习时间：** 10–15 分钟 · **美式自然口语** · A2–B1+

## Today's Goal

Learn how to ask about a day pass, explain what kind of workspace you need, and book a room for a video call. The goal is not to sound formal. It's to ask clear, practical questions and keep the conversation going.

## Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Alex walks into a coworking space. Alex wants somewhere to work for the day and has a video call in the afternoon.*

**1. Receptionist:** Hey! Welcome in. What can I do for you?

**Alex:** Hi. I'm looking for a place to work for the day. Do you offer day passes?

**2. Receptionist:** Yeah, we do. Is this your first time here?

**Alex:** It is. I just need a desk, reliable Wi-Fi, and somewhere I can take a call.

**3. Receptionist:** No problem. The day pass gives you access to the shared workspace and the common areas.

**Alex:** Sounds good. Can I just drop in, or do I need to book ahead?

**4. Receptionist:** You can usually walk in, but booking ahead is a good idea when we're busy.

**Alex:** Got it. I have a video meeting after lunch, so I might need a quieter space.

**5. Receptionist:** In that case, we have phone booths and small meeting rooms. The phone booths are for quick calls, and the meeting rooms are better for longer meetings.

**Alex:** Oh, that's helpful. Is there a screen in the meeting room? I may need to share my screen with the team.

**6. Receptionist:** Some rooms have a display you can connect your laptop to. I can check which ones are available.

**Alex:** Great. Could I book a small room for around half an hour this afternoon?

**7. Receptionist:** Sure. What time are you thinking?

**Alex:** Around two, if possible. Is anything available then?

**8. Receptionist:** The room is booked at two, but there's a slot from 2:15 to 2:45. Would that work?

**Alex:** Yeah, that works for me. Is there an extra charge for using the meeting room?

**9. Receptionist:** There is. The day pass covers the shared area, and meeting rooms are charged separately. I'll show you the rate before I confirm the booking.

**Alex:** Perfect. I'd rather check the total first. Does the room come with an HDMI cable, or should I bring my own?

**10. Receptionist:** There's an HDMI cable in the room, and the display also supports wireless connection.

**Alex:** Nice. How do I get the guest Wi-Fi details?

**11. Receptionist:** I'll put them on your pass receipt. If you have any trouble connecting, just come back to the front desk.

**Alex:** Thanks. I'll get here a few minutes early and test the connection before the call.

**12. Receptionist:** Good idea. And if your meeting runs over, check with us before staying longer. We can extend the booking if the room is free.

**Alex:** Got it. I'll book 2:15 to 2:45, then. Thanks for your help!

## 自然转场为什么成立？

Alex 原本只想买一张 day pass，解决“今天在哪里工作”的问题。第 4 轮，Alex 补充了一个具体细节：午饭后有视频会议，而且需要安静的地方。

这个细节自然引出第二个话题：

- **Topic A：** 是否提供日票、能否直接进场、日票包含什么。
- **Topic B：** 会议室是否空闲、是否收费、有没有屏幕和线缆、会议超时怎么办。

不是突然换场景，而是从“我要在这里工作一天”推进到“我怎样顺利完成今天的一场会议”。

**特别记住：**

- **drop in**：强调不提前预约、直接过去。
- **book ahead**：提前预订，尤其适合会议室、餐厅和活动。
- **run over**：不仅能说会议超时，也能说活动或课程超时。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个可复用句型

### 1. Do you offer ...?

询问一个地方是否提供某种服务。

- Do you offer day passes?
- Do you offer student discounts?
- Do you offer hourly bookings?

中文：你们提供……吗？

### 2. Is there somewhere I can ...?

询问有没有地方可以完成某件事。

- Is there somewhere I can take a call?
- Is there somewhere I can charge my laptop?
- Is there somewhere I can sit quietly?

中文：有没有什么地方可以让我……？

### 3. Would it be possible to ...?

礼貌地提出请求或询问是否可行。比直接命令自然，也不需要说得很正式。

- Would it be possible to book a room at two?
- Would it be possible to stay a little longer?
- Would it be possible to get a quieter desk?

中文：能不能……？是否可以……？

## Pronunciation｜两个自然语流技巧

### 1. Do you → /dʒə/（非重读时的常见弱读）

在美式自然语速中，**do you** 经常连在一起，听起来接近 /dʒə/。

- Do you offer day passes?
- Do you have a meeting room?
- Do you take card payments?

不要把每个词都读得很重。把重音放在 offer、meeting room、card payments 等关键信息上。

### 2. Could you → /kʊdʒə/（连读）

- Could you check the availability?
- Could you show me the room?
- Could you send me the Wi-Fi details?

练习时把 could you 连起来说，语气放松。整句话的重点落在 check、show、send 上。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设前台说：

**Receptionist:** Some rooms have a display you can connect your laptop to.

不要只回答 **Okay, thanks.**。试着抓住 display 这个细节继续问：

1. **React:** Oh, that's helpful.
2. **Pick up a detail:** You mentioned that the room has a display.
3. **Ask a follow-up:** Does it support wireless connection, or should I bring an HDMI cable?

把它连成一段自然表达：

*Oh, that's helpful. You mentioned that the room has a display. Does it support wireless connection, or should I bring an HDMI cable?*

### 再练一个延伸

**Receptionist:** You can extend the booking if the room is free.

**You:** That's good to know. How would I check whether it's available if my meeting runs over?

要点：先回应对方的信息，再抓住一个具体名词或条件，最后问一个有实际价值的问题。这样对话就不会停在 yes、no 或 okay。

## Speaking Challenge｜连续角色扮演

你扮演 Alex，我扮演 coworking space 前台。每轮尽量回答 3–5 句话，并至少主动提出一个 follow-up question。

### Round 1 — Getting a Day Pass

**Receptionist:** Hey! Welcome in. Are you looking for a place to work today?

你的任务：

- 询问是否提供 day pass；
- 说明你需要办公桌和稳定的 Wi-Fi；
- 询问能否直接进来，还是应该提前预约；
- 顺便问一下日票包含哪些区域。

### Round 2 — Finding a Quiet Place for a Call

**Receptionist:** We have phone booths and meeting rooms. What kind of space do you need?

你的任务：

- 说明午饭后有一场视频会议；
- 询问 phone booth 和 meeting room 有什么区别；
- 说明可能需要共享屏幕；
- 询问下午两点左右是否有空的会议室，以及是否额外收费。

### Round 3 — Confirming the Booking

**Receptionist:** I can offer you a room from 2:15 to 2:45. It has a display and an HDMI cable.

你的任务：

- 确认时间；
- 询问如何连接 Wi-Fi 和显示屏；
- 询问如果会议超时能否延长；
- 自然结束对话，并说明你会提前几分钟到。
