# Day 37 — At a Hair Salon → Explaining How You Want to Style Your Hair

**今日话题：** 在理发店描述自己想要的发型 → 询问日常如何打理、造型和安排下次修剪

**Today's goal:** Explain what kind of haircut you want, make small adjustments during the consultation, and ask how to style and maintain it at home.

## Real Conversation｜原创对话

*Situation: Alex walks into a neighborhood hair salon for a haircut. Morgan is the stylist. Alex wants a change, but not anything too dramatic.*

### Topic A — Explaining the Haircut You Want

**1. Morgan:** Hey! What are we thinking today? Just a trim, or are you looking for a different style?

**Alex:** A bit of both, I guess. I want to clean it up, but I don't want to lose too much length.

**2. Morgan:** Got it. How much would you like me to take off?

**Alex:** Maybe just an inch or so. The sides are getting a little bulky, though.

**3. Morgan:** Okay. Do you want the sides shorter, or would you rather keep some length and add a bit of shape?

**Alex:** I'd like them a little shorter, but nothing too close to the skin. Something pretty natural.

**4. Morgan:** Makes sense. Do you usually style your hair in the morning, or do you prefer something low-maintenance?

**Alex:** Honestly, I don't spend much time on it. I usually just towel-dry it and head out.

**5. Morgan:** Then I'd avoid a cut that needs a lot of product. Do you have a photo of what you have in mind?

**Alex:** Yeah, here's one. I like the shape, but I don't want it quite as short on the sides.

**6. Morgan:** I see what you mean. We can keep the top a little longer and blend the sides in more gradually. How does that sound?

**Alex:** That sounds good. Could you leave a little more length in the front, just in case I want to style it differently?

### Topic B — Styling and Looking After the Haircut

**7. Morgan:** Absolutely. I'll leave some room at the front. One thing to keep in mind is that your hair naturally falls forward, so it'll be easy to style that way.

**Alex:** That's helpful. What's the easiest way to style it in the morning?

**8. Morgan:** After washing it, towel-dry it until it's slightly damp. Then use a small amount of styling cream and work it through with your fingers.

**Alex:** Do I need a hair dryer, or can I just let it air-dry?

**9. Morgan:** You can let it air-dry. A hair dryer will give you a bit more volume, but it's not necessary for this style.

**Alex:** Nice. I'd rather keep the routine simple. How often should I come back for a trim?

**10. Morgan:** I'd say every six to eight weeks, depending on how quickly your hair grows and how neat you want the sides to look.

**Alex:** Okay, that sounds manageable. And if I want to keep the top a little longer, should I mention that next time?

**11. Morgan:** Definitely. Just tell me you want to keep the length on top and tidy up the sides. We can adjust it as we go.

**Alex:** Great. Could you also tell me what kind of product to use? I don't want anything that makes my hair look too shiny.

**12. Morgan:** I'd go with a light styling cream or matte paste. Start with a tiny amount—you can always add more.

**Alex:** Perfect. That's exactly what I was looking for. Thanks for walking me through it.

### 自然转场为什么成立？

Topic A 先把发型要求说清楚：想剪短多少、两侧要多短、顶部和前面要保留多少长度，以及平时愿意花多少时间打理。

第 4 轮里，发型师问 Alex 早上是否会认真造型。Alex 说自己通常只是擦干头发就出门。这个细节让发型师意识到：发型不能只在理发店里好看，还要适合 Alex 的日常习惯。

因此自然进入 Topic B：如何吹干、是否需要造型产品、多久修剪一次，以及下次怎样向发型师描述想保留的长度。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个高频句型

### 1. I'd like to ..., but I don't want to ...

表达想要的变化，同时说明自己的边界。

- I'd like to go a little shorter, but I don't want it too short.
- I'd like to change the style, but I don't want anything dramatic.
- I'd like to add some shape, but I don't want to lose the length.

中文：我想……，但我不希望……

### 2. Could you leave ... a little ...?

要求对方在某个部位多留或少剪一点。

- Could you leave the top a little longer?
- Could you leave a little more length around the front?
- Could you keep the sides a little fuller?

中文：你能不能把……稍微留得……

### 3. What's the easiest way to ...?

询问简单、可执行的方法。

- What's the easiest way to style it in the morning?
- What's the easiest way to keep it looking neat?
- What's the easiest way to use this product?

中文：做……最简单的方法是什么？

## Pronunciation｜2 个自然美式发音技巧

### 1. a little：中间的 /t/ 常变成闪音 /ɾ/

美式英语里，非重读元音之间的 /t/ 常听起来像很轻的 d 音：

- a little → /ə ˈlɪɾəl/
- a little shorter → /ə ˈlɪɾəl ˈʃɔrtər/
- a little more → /ə ˈlɪɾəl mɔr/

练习时不要把 little 里的 t 发得很重，也不要在每个词之间停顿。

### 2. Could you：自然融合

在连贯口语里，could you 常接近 /kʊdʒə/：

- Could you leave it longer?
- Could you take a little off?
- Could you show me how to style it?

把重音放在 LEAVE、TAKE、SHOW 上，could you 轻轻带过即可。

## US / UK Note｜美式与英式表达

| American English | British English | 中文 |

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设发型师说：

**Morgan:** A hair dryer will give you a bit more volume, but it's not necessary for this style.

不要只回答 “Okay”。用三步把对话继续下去：

1. **React:** That's good to know.  
   这样我就知道了。

2. **Pick up a detail:** You mentioned it would add more volume.  
   你刚才提到吹风机会让头发更蓬松。

3. **Ask a follow-up:** Would a little bit of mousse work too, or would that be too much?  
   那少量摩丝也可以吗，还是会太多？

连起来说：

*That's good to know. You mentioned it would add more volume. Would a little bit of mousse work too, or would that be too much?*

关键不是连续抛出很多问题，而是抓住对方刚刚说的一个细节，先回应，再顺着它追问。

## Speaking Challenge｜3 个连续角色扮演问题

你扮演 Alex，我扮演发型师 Morgan。每轮尽量说 3–5 句话，并至少提出一个 follow-up question。

### Round 1 — Explain What You Want

**Morgan:** Hey! What kind of haircut are you looking for today?

你的任务：
- 说自己想稍微修剪，但不想剪太短。
- 描述两侧、顶部和前面的长度。
- 解释你平时不太花时间打理头发。
- 询问发型师是否有适合你的建议。

### Round 2 — Make a Small Adjustment

**Morgan:** I can make the sides shorter and keep the top longer. Does that sound good?

你的任务：
- 回应这个建议。
- 说明你希望两侧自然一点，不要太贴头皮。
- 要求前面多留一点长度。
- 追问这样是否容易在早上打理。

### Round 3 — Ask About Daily Styling

**Morgan:** You can use a little styling cream and let it air-dry. It should be pretty easy to manage.

你的任务：
- 回应这个建议。
- 追问是否需要吹风机。
- 询问应该用哪种产品，以及多久修剪一次。
- 用自然的英语总结你今天希望保持的发型。
