# Day 40 — Sorting Household Waste → Arranging a Bulky-Waste Pickup

**今日话题：** 在公寓里确认垃圾分类规则 → 因为要处理一件旧家具，自然延伸到预约大件垃圾回收（德国常说 Sperrmüll）。

**练习时间：** 10–15 分钟 · **美式自然口语** · A2–B1+

## Today's Goal

Learn how to ask a neighbor about shared recycling bins, check where different items belong, and find out how to arrange a bulky-waste pickup. The aim is to sound friendly and practical, not overly formal.

> 小提醒：德国垃圾分类和 Sperrmüll（大件垃圾）规则因城市和地方服务而异。对话中的日期、地点和流程都是虚构示例；实际处理前应查看当地市政网站或物业通知，不要默认所有城市规则相同。

## Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Mia has recently moved into an apartment building. She meets her neighbor, Ben, near the shared bins. Mia also has an old bookshelf she needs to get rid of.*

### Topic A — Sorting Household Waste

**1. Mia:** Hey, do you live in this building?

**Ben:** Yeah, I do. Welcome! Are you new here?

**Mia:** Thanks! I moved in last week. I'm still figuring out how everything works.

**2. Ben:** How's the new place so far?

**Mia:** Pretty good, actually. I just have one question. Which bin should I use for cardboard boxes?

**3. Ben:** The blue one is usually for paper and cardboard here. Just make sure the boxes are flat and don't have a lot of plastic on them.

**Mia:** Got it. What about plastic packaging? Does that go in the yellow bin?

**4. Ben:** Right. The yellow bin is for packaging, but the exact rules can depend on the local system. If you're not sure, there's a guide on the notice board.

**Mia:** That's helpful. I don't want to put the wrong thing in the wrong bin.

**5. Ben:** No worries. It takes a little time to get used to it. The black bin is for things that don't belong in the recycling bins.

**Mia:** Okay. And where should I put glass bottles?

**6. Ben:** There's a public glass container around the corner. It's not inside our bin area. You'll see separate openings for different colors.

**Mia:** Oh, I hadn't noticed that. I'll check it out next time I go shopping.

### Topic B — Arranging a Bulky-Waste Pickup

**7. Ben:** Are you getting rid of anything else, by the way? I saw a bookshelf near your door.

**Mia:** Yeah, that's actually why I wanted to ask. I have an old bookshelf that won't fit in my new room. Can I leave it next to the bins?

**8. Ben:** I'd check first. Large items usually count as bulky waste, and you normally can't just leave them there.

**Mia:** That makes sense. Do you know how we arrange a pickup?

**9. Ben:** The city may offer a scheduled collection, or you might need to book a pickup online. The details should be on the city's waste website.

**Mia:** Okay. Do I need to pay for it, or is it included in the regular waste service?

**10. Ben:** It depends on the city and the type of service. I'd check the current rules before booking.

**Mia:** Fair enough. And do they tell you where to put the furniture on collection day?

**11. Ben:** Usually, the instructions explain the collection point and when you can put it outside. Don't leave it out too early, though.

**Mia:** Good to know. I'll check the website and see what dates are available.

**12. Ben:** If you want, I can show you the notice board later. It has the local waste information, too.

**Mia:** That'd be great, thanks. I appreciate it. I'm still learning how things work around here!

## 自然转场为什么成立？

Mia 一开始只是想确认纸箱、塑料包装和玻璃瓶应该放进哪个垃圾桶。第 7 轮，Ben 注意到她门口有一个旧书架，于是 Mia 解释自己还需要处理一件大件家具。

这个细节自然把对话从日常垃圾分类推进到大件垃圾回收：

- **Topic A：** 纸箱、包装、普通垃圾和玻璃分别放在哪里。
- **Topic B：** 旧书架是否属于 bulky waste、能否预约回收、是否收费，以及回收当天应该放在哪里。

不是突然换场景，而是从“怎样处理日常垃圾”延伸到“怎样正确处理一件不能放进普通垃圾桶的东西”。

**特别记住三个：**

- **sort out**：不仅能表示分类，也能表示把某件事情处理好，例如 *I need to sort out my paperwork.*
- **get rid of**：日常口语中很常用，表示想把不需要的东西处理掉。
- **book a pickup**：预约上门收取，适用于大件垃圾、快递或某些维修服务。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个可复用句型

### 1. Which bin should I use for ...?

询问某样东西应该放进哪个垃圾桶。

- Which bin should I use for cardboard?
- Which bin should I use for glass bottles?
- Which bin should I use for food waste?

中文：……应该用哪个垃圾桶？

### 2. Does this count as ...?

确认某件东西是否属于某一类别。

- Does this count as bulky waste?
- Does this count as plastic packaging?
- Does this count as general waste?

中文：这个算作……吗？

### 3. Do I need to ... or can I ...?

在两个处理方式之间确认规则。

- Do I need to book a pickup, or can I take it to a recycling center?
- Do I need to pay for it, or is it included?
- Do I need to put it outside the night before, or can I do it in the morning?

中文：我需要……，还是可以……？

## Pronunciation｜两个自然语流技巧

### 1. Should I → /ʃʊdʒaɪ/

在自然美式语速中，**should I** 常连起来，听起来接近 /ʃʊdʒaɪ/。

- Which bin should I use?
- Should I book a pickup?
- Should I check the website first?

练习时不要把 should 和 I 分得太开。句子的重音放在 **BIN、USE、BOOK、WEBSITE** 等信息词上。

### 2. Have to → /ˈhæftə/

在很多美式口语语境中，**have to** 的 /v/ 会受到后面 /t/ 的影响，听起来接近 /hæftə/。

- I have to check the local rules.
- Do I have to book ahead?
- You have to put the boxes in the right bin.

注意：这是自然语流中的常见发音，不需要每次刻意把 /v/ 改成 /f/；先听熟，再在跟读中模仿。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设邻居告诉你：

**Ben:** The city may offer a scheduled collection, or you might need to book a pickup online.

不要只回答 **Okay, thanks.** 试着抓住 scheduled collection 这个细节继续问：

1. **React:** Oh, that's good to know.  
   哦，这个信息很有用。

2. **Pick up a detail:** You mentioned that I might need to book it online.  
   你提到我可能需要在线预约。

3. **Ask a follow-up:** Do you know where I can find the booking page, and how far in advance should I arrange it?  
   你知道我在哪里可以找到预约页面吗？我需要提前多久安排？

把三个步骤连起来：

*Oh, that's good to know. You mentioned that I might need to book it online. Do you know where I can find the booking page, and how far in advance should I arrange it?*

### 再练一个延伸

**Ben:** Don't leave it out too early. The collection instructions will tell you when to put it outside.

**You:** That makes sense. Is there a specific time window, or do they give you a date?

关键：回应对方 → 抓住一个具体细节 → 问一个能推动对话的问题。不要只用 *Okay* 结束交流。

## Speaking Challenge｜连续角色扮演

你扮演 Mia，我扮演住在同一栋楼里的邻居 Ben。每轮尽量回答 3–5 句话，并至少主动提出一个 follow-up question。

### Round 1 — Asking About the Bins

**Ben:** Hey! Are you new to the building? Do you need a hand with anything?

你的任务：

- 说自己上周刚搬进来；
- 询问纸箱、塑料包装和玻璃瓶分别应该放在哪里；
- 追问公告栏上是否有分类说明；
- 不要只说 thank you，继续问一个具体问题。

### Round 2 — Getting Rid of an Old Bookshelf

**Ben:** I noticed that old bookshelf near your door. Are you planning to keep it?

你的任务：

- 解释书架太大，放不进新房间；
- 询问它是否属于 bulky waste；
- 询问能否直接放在垃圾桶旁边；
- 追问是否需要在线预约回收。

### Round 3 — Confirming the Pickup

**Ben:** You may be able to book a collection through the city's website.

你的任务：

- 先回应这个信息；
- 询问在哪里可以找到预约页面；
- 询问是否收费、什么时候可以把书架搬出去；
- 自然结束对话，并感谢邻居的帮助。
