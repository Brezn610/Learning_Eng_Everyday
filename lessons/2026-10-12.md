# Day 02 — What Do You Study? → What Are You Working On? 🎓

**今日话题：大学专业 → 日常项目与困难**  
**目标：** 用普通英语介绍专业、项目、进展及困难，避免把聊天讲成技术报告。

## 1. Real Conversation｜原创对话

*Situation: You meet someone at a university event.*

1. **Alex:** Hey, are you a student here?  
   **You:** Yeah, I'm doing my master's in AI.
2. **Alex:** Oh, that's interesting. What exactly do you study?  
   **You:** Mostly machine learning and how computers make sense of the world.
3. **Alex:** Is it more about programming or engineering?  
   **You:** A bit of both, actually.
4. **Alex:** So what do you spend most of your time doing?  
   **You:** These days, I'm mostly working on a research project.
5. **Alex:** Oh, really? What's it about?  
   **You:** Basically, we're trying to make a repetitive task easier with AI.
6. **Alex:** Sounds useful. How's it going so far?  
   **You:** Pretty well, but there are a few issues we're trying to figure out.
7. **Alex:** Like what?  
   **You:** Sometimes the system works well on easy examples but struggles with harder ones.
8. **Alex:** I see. How do you deal with that?  
   **You:** We try different approaches, run experiments, and compare results.
9. **Alex:** Do you work by yourself?  
   **You:** Mostly, but I also discuss ideas with my supervisor.
10. **Alex:** That must take a lot of time.  
    **You:** It does. Sometimes I spend hours figuring out a bug.
11. **Alex:** I know that feeling. Do you ever get stuck?  
    **You:** All the time! Taking a break usually helps.
12. **Alex:** So, do you enjoy it?  
    **You:** Definitely. It's frustrating sometimes, but I really enjoy solving problems.

**自然转场：** 从“学什么专业”继续问“你平时具体在做什么”，再问“这个项目进展怎么样”。专业内容由具体工作连接起来。

## 2. Key Vocabulary & Chunks

| 词汇／语块 | 美式 IPA | 中文 | 简单例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| work on | /wɝːk ɑːn/ | 正在做／改进 | I'm working on a project. |
| figure out | /ˈfɪɡ.jɚ aʊt/ | 弄明白 | I'm trying to figure it out. |
| approach | /əˈproʊtʃ/ | 方法、思路 | Let's try another approach. |
| feedback | /ˈfiːd.bæk/ | 反馈 | I got some helpful feedback. |
| get stuck | /ɡet stʌk/ | 卡住 | I get stuck sometimes. |
| deal with | /diːl wɪð/ | 应对、处理 | How do you deal with stress? |
| so far | /soʊ fɑːr/ | 到目前为止 | It's going well so far. |
| frustrating | /ˈfrʌs.treɪ.tɪŋ/ | 让人沮丧的 | Debugging can be frustrating. |
| take a break | /teɪk ə breɪk/ | 休息一会儿 | Let's take a short break. |

### 三个可复用句型

- **I'm working on…** — 我最近正在做…… *I'm working on improving my English.*
- **I'm trying to figure out…** — 我正试着弄清楚…… *I'm trying to figure out why it failed.*
- **How do you deal with…?** — 你如何处理…… *How do you deal with difficult problems?*

## 3. Pronunciation｜发音练习

- **What do you…?** 在美式快语速中，*do you* 常弱化，整句可以连起来说；先说清楚，再提速。
- **working on**：/ˈwɝː.kɪŋ ɑːn/，*working‿on* 连读。

## 4. Conversation Skill｜React + Follow-up

对方：*I've been having trouble with my project.*  
自然回应：*Oh, that sounds frustrating. What's been the biggest problem?*

先表达理解，再问更具体的问题，比连续机械提问自然得多。

## 5. Speaking Challenge｜开口练习

1. **What are you studying, and why did you choose it?**
2. **What are you working on these days?** 尽量用非技术人员听得懂的英语回答。
3. **What's been the biggest challenge so far?** 说明问题，再解释你如何处理，最后反问对方。

## 6. Listening｜拓展听力

可在 [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish) 搜索 **Real Easy English** 的工作话题或 **English at Work**。本篇对话为原创；未在此声明未经重新验证的单期链接。

**Takeaway：** *I'm working on… / I'm trying to figure out… / How's it going so far?*
