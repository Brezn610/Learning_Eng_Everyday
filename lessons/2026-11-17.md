# Day 38 — At the Pharmacy → Checking the Label Before You Leave

**今日话题：** 在药房询问非处方过敏药 → 看懂药品标签、确认副作用，并在开车前问清注意事项

**Today's goal:** Explain what you're looking for, ask a pharmacist clear questions, and check the product information instead of guessing.

> 本课是英语情境练习，不提供个人用药建议。具体产品是否适合、如何使用以及能否与其他药物同服，应向药剂师或医生确认，并以该产品的说明书和标签为准。

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Jamie visits a local pharmacy because seasonal allergies are bothering them. Jamie wants to ask about an over-the-counter option and has to drive later that afternoon.*

### Topic A — Asking for Allergy Relief

**1. Pharmacist:** Hi there. What can I help you with today?  
**Jamie:** Hi. I'm looking for something for hay fever. Do you have anything over the counter?

**2. Pharmacist:** Sure. What symptoms are you having?  
**Jamie:** Mostly sneezing, a runny nose, and itchy eyes. It's been bothering me for a few days.

**3. Pharmacist:** I see. Have you tried anything for it yet?  
**Jamie:** Not this time. I'd like to ask what's available before I choose something.

**4. Pharmacist:** That's fine. Are you taking any regular medication, or do you have anything you'd like me to know before I recommend a product?  
**Jamie:** I do take a regular prescription. I don't have the details with me, but I can check the name on my phone.

**5. Pharmacist:** Great. It's important to check that before choosing anything. Is there anything else that matters to you?  
**Jamie:** Yes. I'd prefer something that won't make me sleepy. I need to drive this afternoon.

**6. Pharmacist:** Thanks for mentioning that. Some options are less likely to cause drowsiness, but people can react differently. Let's check the information on the package together.  
**Jamie:** Good idea. Could you show me where it says whether it may cause drowsiness?

### Topic B — Checking the Instructions Before Driving

**7. Pharmacist:** Sure. Look under the warnings and precautions section. It explains what to watch out for and whether you should avoid driving.  
**Jamie:** Got it. I don't want to miss anything important. How should I check the instructions for taking it?

**8. Pharmacist:** Start with the directions on this specific package. The instructions can vary from one product to another, so don't assume they're all the same.  
**Jamie:** That makes sense. Should I take it with food, or does the label say?

**9. Pharmacist:** Let's check the label rather than guess. It also tells you how often to use the product and what the recommended amount is.  
**Jamie:** Thanks. And can I take it together with a cold medicine if I need one later?

**10. Pharmacist:** Please check with a pharmacist or doctor before combining medicines. Some products can contain similar ingredients or have interactions.  
**Jamie:** Okay, I'll ask first instead of mixing anything on my own.

**11. Pharmacist:** Good plan. If your symptoms get worse, don't improve, or you're unsure what to do, seek medical advice.  
**Jamie:** Understood. Could I get a smaller pack first? I'd like to read everything carefully.

**12. Pharmacist:** Of course. Here's the smaller pack. Before you leave, make sure you've read the warnings. Don't drive if the label says not to, or if you feel drowsy.  
**Jamie:** Thanks. I'll check the label before using it and make sure I'm okay to drive.

### 自然转场为什么成立？

Jamie 一开始只是想买一种缓解花粉症状的非处方产品。第 5 轮，Jamie 提到下午还要开车，这个细节改变了接下来的问题：不能只问“哪一种有效”，还要确认是否会引起困倦。

因此话题自然从 **选择产品（Topic A）** 转到 **阅读标签、确认副作用和驾驶注意事项（Topic B）**。这是同一件事的下一步，不是突然换场景。

1. **Is this available over the counter?**  
   这个可以直接作为非处方产品购买吗？

2. **Does it cause drowsiness?**

3. **Can I take it with my regular medication?**  
   我可以把它和我平时使用的药物一起服用吗？

## ③ Reusable Patterns｜3 个高频句型

### Pattern 1 — I'm looking for something for ...

在商店、药房或服务场景中说明自己需要什么。

- I'm looking for something for hay fever.
- I'm looking for something for a sore throat.
- I'm looking for something to help with a runny nose.

中文：我想找一种用于……的东西。

### Pattern 2 — I'd prefer something that ...

表达偏好或限制条件。

- I'd prefer something that won't make me sleepy.
- I'd prefer something that's easy to use.
- I'd prefer something without a strong smell.

中文：我更希望有一种……的东西。

### Pattern 3 — Just so I don't get this wrong, ...

在重要信息上再次确认，避免误解。

- Just so I don't get this wrong, should I check with you before combining medicines?
- Just so I don't get this wrong, I should follow the instructions on this package?
- Just so I don't get this wrong, the warning about driving is on the label?

中文：为了避免我理解错，我确认一下……

## ④ Pronunciation｜两个自然美式发音技巧

### 1. Do you 在自然语流中的弱读

日常对话里，非重读的 **do you** 经常连得很紧，听起来接近 /dʒə/。

- Do you have anything over the counter?
- Do you take it with food?
- Do you have the instructions?

练习时不要把每个词都读得一样重，重点放在 **HAVE、COUNTER、FOOD、INSTRUCTIONS**。

### 2. Can I 的连读

**Can I** 在自然语速下常连成 /kəˈnaɪ/，而不是两个完全分开的词。

- Can I take it with food?
- Can I ask one more question?
- Can I check the label first?

把 can 弱读，重音放在 I 后面的动词或信息词上。

### 美式与英式表达

| American English | British English | 中文 |

| pharmacy / drugstore | pharmacy / chemist's | 药房 |
| pharmacist | pharmacist / chemist | 药剂师 |

## ⑤ Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设药剂师说：

**Pharmacist:** Some options are less likely to cause drowsiness, but people can react differently.

不要只回答 **Okay**。试试三个步骤：

**Step 1 — React**

*Thanks for pointing that out.*

谢谢你特别提醒这一点。

**Step 2 — Pick up a detail**

*You mentioned that drowsiness can still happen.*

你提到仍然有可能出现困倦。

**Step 3 — Ask a follow-up**

*Where can I check that on the package, and what should I look for before driving?*

我可以在包装的哪里确认这一点？开车之前应该留意什么？

把三个步骤自然连起来：

*Thanks for pointing that out. You mentioned that drowsiness can still happen. Where can I check that on the package, and what should I look for before driving?*

关键是先回应，再抓住对方说的具体细节，最后问一个真正有用的问题。

## ⑦ Speaking Challenge｜三个连续角色扮演问题

你扮演 Jamie，我扮演 Pharmacist。每轮尽量说 3–5 句话，并主动提出一个 follow-up question。

### Round 1 — Ask for Something Over the Counter

**Pharmacist:** Hi there. What can I help you with today?

你的任务：

- 说明你想找一种缓解花粉症状的非处方产品。
- 描述 sneezing、runny nose 和 itchy eyes。
- 询问是否有合适的选择。
- 说明自己希望先了解产品，再决定购买。

### Round 2 — Ask About Side Effects

**Pharmacist:** Some products may cause drowsiness. Are you taking any regular medication?

你的任务：

- 说明你下午需要开车。
- 询问是否有不容易引起困倦的选择。
- 说明自己有规律使用的处方药，需要确认是否会相互影响。
- 追问应该在包装的哪里查看副作用和警告。

### Round 3 — Check the Instructions

**Pharmacist:** Here's the package. The instructions and warnings are on the label.

你的任务：

- 请药剂师解释说明书上的使用方法。
- 询问是否需要与食物一起使用，但不要自己猜测。
- 询问如果症状没有改善应该怎么办。
- 确认开车前需要注意的警告，并自然结束对话。
