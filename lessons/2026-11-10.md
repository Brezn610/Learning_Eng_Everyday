# Day 31 — Booking a Restaurant Table → Checking Vegetarian Options

**今日话题：** 预订餐厅座位 → 确认素食选择并调整点餐细节

## Real Conversation｜原创对话

*Situation: Jamie calls a neighborhood restaurant to book a table for four on Friday evening. One friend doesn't eat meat, so Jamie also checks the menu.*

**1. Jamie:** Hi, do you have a table for four this Friday around seven?

**Host:** Let me check. We have seven or seven thirty. Which works better for you?

**2. Jamie:** Seven would be great, if that's available.

**Host:** Sure. Can I get a name for the reservation?

**3. Jamie:** It's Jamie. And could we have a table inside, if possible?

**Host:** Of course. I'll put that in the notes. Is there anything else I should know?

**4. Jamie:** Actually, one of my friends is vegetarian. Do you have a few vegetarian options?

**Host:** Yes, we do. We have a mushroom risotto and a roasted vegetable pasta.

**5. Jamie:** Nice. Does the risotto come with any meat or meat stock?

**Host:** Let me double-check with the kitchen. I believe it's made with vegetable stock, but I'll confirm.

**6. Jamie:** Thanks. I'd rather make sure before we get there.

**Host:** Absolutely. The chef has confirmed it's vegetarian. I can also add a note to your reservation.

**自然转场为什么成立？** 预订座位时，Jamie 提到有一位朋友吃素。这个信息会影响整桌人的点餐安排，因此对话自然从“有没有位置、几点到”延伸到“菜单里有什么、菜品具体用什么食材”。这不是换场景，而是在补全同一顿晚餐的安排。

**7. Jamie:** That would be helpful. Does the vegetable pasta come with cheese?

**Host:** Yes, it normally does, but we can leave it off if your friend prefers.

**8. Jamie:** Great. And could we get the dressing on the side for the salad?

**Host:** Sure, that's no problem.

**9. Jamie:** Perfect. I think the risotto and the pasta should give them a couple of options.

**Host:** Definitely. We can also go over the menu with you when you arrive.

**10. Jamie:** Sounds good. Just to confirm, that's a table for four at seven on Friday, under Jamie?

**Host:** That's right. I'll add a note that one guest is vegetarian.

**11. Jamie:** Great. Could you also make a note that we'd prefer an indoor table?

**Host:** Already done. You're all set.

**12. Jamie:** Perfect. Thanks for your help. We'll see you Friday.

**Host:** You're welcome. Have a good evening!

## Reusable Patterns｜三个高频句型

### 1. Do you have a table for [number] at [time]?

用于电话或现场预订。

- Do you have a table for two at six?
- Do you have a table for five around eight?

中文：你们……点有……个人的桌位吗？

### 2. Does that come with ...?

确认一道菜包含什么配菜、酱料或食材。

- Does that come with rice?
- Does the salad come with dressing?
- Does the pasta come with cheese?

中文：这个包含……吗？／这个会配……吗？

### 3. Could I get ... on the side / without ...?

礼貌地提出点餐调整。

- Could I get the sauce on the side?
- Could I get that without onions?
- Could we have the cheese on the side?

中文：可以把……另外放吗？／可以不要……吗？

## Pronunciation｜两个自然美式发音技巧

### 1. Could I → /kʊd aɪ/

在 *Could I get a table...?* 里，两个词会自然连在一起。不要在 *could* 和 *I* 之间停顿。

练习：
- Could I get a table for four?
- Could I see the menu?
- Could I have some water?

### 2. For a → /fɚ ə/

在 *a table for a party of four* 这样的短语里，*for* 和 *a* 都比较轻。美式英语常把它们连起来，听起来接近 /fərə/。

练习：
- a table for a couple
- a table for a party of four
- Is seven okay for a party of four?

## US / UK Note｜美英差异

- **reservation** 在美国很常用；英国也听得懂，日常还常说 **booking**。
- **check** 在美国餐厅通常指账单；英国更常说 **bill**。
- 美国餐厅说 **entrée** 通常指主菜；英国菜单里的 **entrée** 有时指前菜，最好结合菜单理解。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设店员说：

**Host:** We have a mushroom risotto and a roasted vegetable pasta.

不要只说 *Okay, thanks.* 试着继续：

1. **React:** Oh, those both sound good.  
   哦，这两种听起来都不错。
2. **Pick up a detail:** So the risotto is one of your vegetarian dishes?  
   所以烩饭是你们的素食菜品之一？
3. **Ask a follow-up:** Is it made with vegetable stock?  
   它是用蔬菜高汤做的吗？

连起来说：

*Oh, those both sound good. So the risotto is one of your vegetarian dishes? Is it made with vegetable stock?*

如果店员回答 *Yes, it is*, 还可以接着问：

*Great. And does it come with a side salad?*

这样就从简单确认菜单，发展成自然、具体的互动。

## Speaking Challenge｜三个连续角色扮演问题

今天你扮演 Jamie，我扮演餐厅工作人员。每轮尽量说 3–5 句话，并且主动追问。

### Round 1 — Make a reservation

**Host:** Thanks for calling. How can I help you?

你的任务：
- 预订周五晚上的四人桌。
- 询问七点是否有位置。
- 提出希望坐室内。

### Round 2 — Ask about the menu

**Host:** We have mushroom risotto and roasted vegetable pasta. Both can be made vegetarian.

你的任务：
- 先回应对方推荐。
- 追问烩饭使用什么高汤。
- 询问意面是否含奶酪，以及能否不加。

### Round 3 — Confirm the details

**Host:** No problem. I've added those notes to your reservation. Is there anything else?

你的任务：
- 确认人数、日期、时间和预订姓名。
- 再确认素食备注和室内座位。
- 用自然的感谢和告别结束通话。
