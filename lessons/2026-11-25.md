# Day 46 — Registering for an Exam → Resolving a Timetable Clash

**今日话题：** 在大学考试系统中解决报名问题 → 发现考试时间冲突，联系考试协调员确认替代安排。

**Today's goal:** Explain a registration problem clearly, ask the right office for help, and confirm who is responsible for each next step.

## Real Conversation｜原创对话

*Situation: Jordan is a student. Casey works at the university's exam office. The portal says a prerequisite is missing even though Jordan passed it last term. Jordan also notices another exam is scheduled at the same time.*

1. **Jordan:** Hi, I'm trying to register for the statistics exam, but the portal says I'm not eligible.  
   **Casey:** Okay, let's see what's going on. Have you registered for the course itself?

2. **Jordan:** Yes, I signed up for the course last month. It's the exam registration that's giving me trouble.  
   **Casey:** Got it. Does the portal show an error message, or is the registration button just greyed out?

3. **Jordan:** It says, “Prerequisite not met.” But I passed that prerequisite last semester.  
   **Casey:** That may mean your result hasn't been transferred to the exam system yet.

4. **Jordan:** Oh, I see. Is that something you can check, or do I need to contact the department?  
   **Casey:** I can log the issue and ask the department to check your record. When did you pass the prerequisite?

5. **Jordan:** Last term. The registration deadline is Friday, so I'm a bit worried about missing it because of a system issue.  
   **Casey:** That's understandable. Could you send us a screenshot of the error? It'll help us look into it.

6. **Jordan:** Sure, I can send it right after this. Is there anything else I should include?  
   **Casey:** Your student number and the course code should be enough. Please don't send any passwords.

7. **Jordan:** No problem. There's actually one more thing. I noticed that this exam overlaps with another exam on Thursday.  
   **Casey:** Ah, that's a separate issue. Are they scheduled at exactly the same time?

8. **Jordan:** Yes, both start at 9 a.m. I only noticed because I checked my timetable again.  
   **Casey:** In that case, you'll need to contact the exam coordinator. The exam office can't approve a timetable change directly.

9. **Jordan:** Should I email the coordinator today and attach both exam schedules?  
   **Casey:** Yes. Explain the conflict clearly and ask whether an alternative sitting is available.

10. **Jordan:** Would they usually move one of the exams, or does it depend on the department?  
    **Casey:** It depends on the university's rules and the department. Don't assume an alternative will be offered until they confirm it.

11. **Jordan:** Got it. Since the portal won't let me register, should I wait for your update before doing anything else?  
    **Casey:** No need to wait to contact the coordinator. I'll log the portal issue today, but I can't promise that the email itself counts as exam registration.

12. **Jordan:** So you'll ask the department to check the prerequisite record, and I'll email the coordinator about the clash. I'll also keep an eye on the Friday deadline.  
    **Casey:** Exactly. Keep the written replies, and let us know if the portal still blocks you tomorrow.

### 自然转场为什么成立？

Jordan 原本只是来解决考试报名失败的问题。检查错误信息时，他提到 prerequisite 和 registration deadline；随后又补充自己重新查看 timetable 时发现两场考试重叠。于是对话从 **fixing an online registration problem** 自然延伸到 **resolving a timetable conflict**。

重点是：在真实行政沟通中，要区分不同负责人和不同流程。Exam office 可以记录系统问题，但时间冲突可能由 exam coordinator 或 department 决定。不要把“发了邮件”误认为“已经完成报名”。

### 重要难句

- **Your result hasn't been transferred to the exam system yet.**  
  你的成绩可能还没有同步到考试系统。
- **The exam office can't approve a timetable change directly.**  
  考试办公室不能直接批准更改考试时间。
- **I can't promise that the email itself counts as exam registration.**  
  我不能保证单纯发邮件就算完成考试报名。
- **Keep the written replies.**  
  把书面回复保存好。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个可复用句型

### 1. I'm trying to..., but...

先说明目标，再说明遇到的障碍。

- I'm trying to submit the form, but the portal keeps rejecting it.
- I'm trying to book an appointment, but there are no available slots.

### 2. Could you check whether...?

礼貌地请对方核实信息。

- Could you check whether my result has been recorded?
- Could you check whether my registration went through?

### 3. So you'll..., and I'll...

确认双方分工，避免会后不知道谁负责什么。

- So you'll contact the department, and I'll email the coordinator.
- So you'll check the payment, and I'll send the receipt.

## Pronunciation｜自然语流

1. **Could you**：口语中常连成接近 /kʊdʒə/ 的声音。练习 *Could you check that for me?*，把重音放在 **check**，不要每个词都读得一样重。
2. **Trying to**：*to* 通常弱读为 /tə/。在 *I'm trying to register* 中，重点放在 **trying** 和 **register**，中间不要逐词停顿。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

**场景一**

**Casey:** The registration deadline is Friday.

- **React:** That's helpful to know.
- **Pick up a detail:** So Friday is the final day to register.
- **Ask a follow-up:** If the portal is still blocked tomorrow, who should I contact?

**场景二**

**Casey:** The exam coordinator handles timetable conflicts.

- **React:** Okay, thanks for clarifying.
- **Pick up a detail:** So the coordinator is the person who deals with exam schedules.
- **Ask a follow-up:** Should I contact them by email or through the student portal?

不要为了追问而追问。先回应对方，再抓住一个具体信息，最后提出真正影响下一步的问题。

## Speaking Challenge｜3 轮连续角色扮演

你扮演 Jordan，我扮演学校工作人员。每轮尽量回答 3–5 句话，并主动提出一个 follow-up question。

### Round 1 — A Registration Problem

**Casey · Exam Office**

*Hi there. What seems to be the problem with your exam registration?*

你的任务：
- 说明系统显示自己不符合报名资格。
- 解释你上学期已经通过先修课程。
- 询问工作人员能否帮你核实成绩记录。

### Round 2 — A Timetable Conflict

**Casey · Exam Office**

*I can log the system issue. You also mentioned another exam at the same time. What's the situation?*

你的任务：
- 解释两场考试的时间冲突。
- 询问应该联系哪个部门。
- 询问是否可能有另一个考试场次。

### Round 3 — Confirming the Next Steps

**Casey · Exam Coordinator**

*Thanks for explaining. What exactly are you asking us to check, and what do you need to know before the deadline?*

你的任务：
- 总结系统报名问题和考试时间冲突。
- 询问邮件是否算正式报名，还是必须在系统中完成。
- 确认自己接下来要联系谁、保存什么记录。
- 用 **So you'll..., and I'll...** 复述双方分工。
