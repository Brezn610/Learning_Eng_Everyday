# Day 35 — Taking a Laptop to a Repair Shop → Discussing Data, Privacy, and the Repair Timeline

**今日话题：** 去维修店描述笔记本电脑的问题 → 进一步确认数据安全、维修费用和取机时间

**Today's goal:** Explain a device problem clearly, ask what the technician needs to do, and agree on the next steps without sharing more information than necessary.

## Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Jamie takes a laptop to a local repair shop. The laptop has been freezing and restarting unexpectedly. Riley is the technician.*

### Topic A — Describing the Problem

**1. Riley:** Hi there. What seems to be the problem with your laptop?

**Jamie:** It’s been acting up for the past few days. It freezes, and sometimes it restarts by itself.

**2. Riley:** Does it happen when you're using a particular app, or is it pretty random?

**Jamie:** Mostly when I have a few things open at once, but it’s happened even when I’m just browsing the web.

**3. Riley:** Got it. Did anything change recently? Maybe a software update or a new program?

**Jamie:** I installed an update last week. I'm not sure if that's related, though.

**4. Riley:** No problem. We can run a diagnostic and see what's going on. Is the laptop still under warranty?

**Jamie:** I don't think so. I bought it a little over three years ago.

**5. Riley:** Okay. The diagnostic fee is €35, and we'll let you know before doing any paid repairs.

**Jamie:** That's fine. Could you give me an estimate before you start fixing anything?

**6. Riley:** Absolutely. We should have an initial result by tomorrow afternoon. Is there anything important on the laptop that you haven't backed up?

**Jamie:** Yeah, I have some files I really don't want to lose.

### Topic B — Protecting Data and Agreeing on Next Steps

**7. Riley:** In that case, we'll check the drive first. We won't erase anything without asking you.

**Jamie:** Thanks. I'd rather not share my login password. Can you run the basic tests without it?

**8. Riley:** Usually, yes. If we need to access your user account, we'll contact you first and explain why.

**Jamie:** That works for me. Should I back up my files before I leave it here?

**9. Riley:** If the laptop still starts, I'd recommend backing up anything important before the repair. If it won't stay on, let us know and we'll discuss the safest option.

**Jamie:** Okay. I can copy the important files to an external drive tonight.

**10. Riley:** Sounds good. Once we know what's wrong, we'll send you a quote and an estimated repair time.

**Jamie:** Great. And if the repair costs more than expected, please check with me before going ahead.

**11. Riley:** Of course. We won't do any extra work without your approval. You can also call us if you want an update.

**Jamie:** Perfect. Would it be realistic to pick it up by Friday?

**12. Riley:** If it's a software issue, probably. If we need to order a part, it might take a little longer.

**Jamie:** No rush, then. Just keep me posted, and I'll wait for your estimate.

### 自然转场为什么成立？

Topic A 先解决“电脑出了什么问题”：什么时候卡顿、是否和更新有关、是否还在保修期内。维修人员接着问有没有重要文件尚未备份。

这个细节自然引出 Topic B：电脑要留在店里，顾客就需要确认数据会不会被清除、是否必须提供密码、维修前是否会先报价，以及什么时候能取机。

转场不是突然换话题，而是从**设备故障**推进到**把设备交给别人维修时需要确认的事情**。

### 重要难句

- **It's been acting up for the past few days.**  
  这几天它一直不太正常／老出小毛病。
- **It freezes, and sometimes it restarts by itself.**  
  它会卡死，有时还会自己重启。
- **We won't erase anything without asking you.**  
  没有先征求你的同意，我们不会删除任何东西。
- **Please check with me before going ahead.**  
  在继续处理之前，请先跟我确认。
- **Just keep me posted.**  
  有进展随时告诉我。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个可复用高频句型

### 1. It's been ... for the past ...

描述一个持续了一段时间的问题或状态。

- It's been freezing for the past two days.
- I've been having trouble with the Wi-Fi for the past week.
- It's been making a strange noise for the past few days.

中文：过去……这段时间一直……

### 2. I'd rather not ... Can you ...?

礼貌表达自己的顾虑，同时提出替代办法。

- I'd rather not share my password. Can you use a guest account?
- I'd rather not replace the whole device. Can you check the battery first?
- I'd rather not pay for extra work without an estimate. Can you call me first?

中文：我不太想……，你能不能……？

### 3. Before you ..., could you ...?

在对方采取行动之前，先确认条件或请求说明。

- Before you replace the part, could you tell me how much it costs?
- Before you erase anything, could you call me?
- Before you start the repair, could you send me a quote?

中文：在你……之前，能不能先……？

## Pronunciation｜2 个自然美式发音技巧

### 1. Could you 的自然融合

在轻松的美式口语里，could you 常融合成 /kʊdʒə/。

练习：

- Could you check the battery?
- Could you give me an estimate?
- Could you call me first?

不要把两个词拆得太开；把重音放在真正重要的动作上，例如 **CHECK**、**ESTIMATE**、**CALL**。

### 2. 连读：keep me posted

这几个词在自然语流中会连得比较紧。练习时保持节奏，不要逐词停顿：

- Keep me posted.
- Let me know if anything changes.
- I'll get back to you tomorrow.

美式口语常把功能词读轻，把信息重点放在 posted、know、changes、tomorrow 上。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

对方说：

**Technician:** We won't erase anything without asking you.

不要只回答 **Okay, thanks.**

试试三步接话：

1. **React:** That's good to know.  
   这样我就放心一些了。
2. **Pick up a detail:** You mentioned checking the drive first.  
   你刚才说会先检查硬盘。
3. **Ask a follow-up:** Would you let me know before doing anything that could affect my files?  
   如果有任何操作可能影响我的文件，你能提前告诉我吗？

连起来练：

*That's good to know. You mentioned checking the drive first. Would you let me know before doing anything that could affect my files?*

这样既回应了对方，也抓住了“检查硬盘”这个细节，并提出了有意义的追问。

## Speaking Challenge｜3 个连续角色扮演问题

你扮演 Jamie，我扮演维修店工作人员 Riley。每轮尽量说 3–5 句话，并主动问一个 follow-up。

### Round 1 — Explain the Problem

**Riley:** Hi! What seems to be wrong with your laptop?

你的任务：
- 说明电脑最近出现了什么问题。
- 说清楚问题通常在什么情况下发生。
- 补充你最近是否安装了更新或新软件。
- 问我能否先做一次检测。

### Round 2 — Ask About Your Data

**Riley:** We may need to keep your laptop overnight. Is there anything important on it?

你的任务：
- 说明哪些文件比较重要。
- 询问是否需要先备份。
- 礼貌表达不想直接分享登录密码。
- 追问维修人员是否会在删除或修改文件前联系你。

### Round 3 — Confirm the Cost and Timeline

**Riley:** The diagnostic is done. It may need a new part. We can send you a quote this afternoon.

你的任务：
- 询问维修总费用和预计时间。
- 说明未经你同意不要开始额外维修。
- 询问什么时候可以取机。
- 用自然的英语结束对话，例如请对方有进展时及时通知你。
