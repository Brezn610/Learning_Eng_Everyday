# Day 03 — Food Shopping → Cooking Habits 🛒

**今日话题：超市买菜 → 做饭和饮食习惯**  
**目标：** 从购物自然转到日常习惯，再聊个人偏好；重点是追问与反问。

## 1. Real Conversation｜原创对话

*Situation: You run into a classmate at the supermarket.*

1. **Alex:** Hey! Didn't expect to see you here. What are you shopping for?  
   **You:** I'm just picking up a few things for dinner.
2. **Alex:** Oh, nice. What are you making?  
   **You:** Probably some beef and rice. Nothing fancy.
3. **Alex:** Do you usually cook for yourself?  
   **You:** Most of the time. I used to eat out a lot, but I've been cooking more lately.
4. **Alex:** Really? What changed?  
   **You:** Eating out gets expensive, and I actually enjoy cooking.
5. **Alex:** Do you plan your meals ahead of time?  
   **You:** Not really. I buy whatever I feel like eating.
6. **Alex:** Same here. I always end up buying too much.  
   **You:** Haha, I know what you mean.
7. **Alex:** What's your go-to meal when you're busy?  
   **You:** Fried rice. It's easy, and I can use whatever is in the fridge.
8. **Alex:** Do you ever cook enough for a few days?  
   **You:** Sometimes. I make extra so I have leftovers.
9. **Alex:** I should do that. I never feel like cooking after a long day.  
   **You:** Same here. Especially when I've been busy all day.
10. **Alex:** That's when I end up ordering takeout.  
    **You:** It's tempting, but I'm trying to cut back on that.
11. **Alex:** Do you cook mostly Chinese food?  
    **You:** Yeah, mostly. It's what I grew up eating.
12. **Alex:** What's one dish you'd recommend?  
    **You:** Dumplings with pickled cabbage. Maybe we could make some together sometime!

**自然转场：** “买什么晚餐”→“通常是否自己做饭”→“忙的时候做什么”→“喜欢什么菜”。没有生硬跳转。

## 2. Key Vocabulary & Chunks

### 三个可复用句型

- **What are you making?** — 你在做什么吃的？ *What are you making for dinner?*
- **Do you usually…?** — 你通常……吗？ *Do you usually cook at home?*
- **I used to…, but now…** — 以前……现在…… *I used to eat out, but now I cook more.*

## 3. Pronunciation｜发音练习

- **gonna** /ˈɡʌnə/：口语中 *going to + 动词* 的常见弱读，*I'm gonna cook tonight.*；不是所有 *going to* 都能缩成 *gonna*。
- **pick up** /pɪk ʌp/：让词尾 /k/ 自然连接后面的元音，练习 *Can you pick up some milk?*

**英美区别：** takeout (US) / takeaway (UK)；eggplant (US) / aubergine (UK)。

## 4. Conversation Skill｜抓住对方提到的变化

**A:** I used to eat out a lot, but now I cook.  
**B:** Oh, really? **What changed?**

然后再进一步问：*What do you usually cook?*

**React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up**。重点是接住前一句的信息，而不是突然切换场景。

## 5. Speaking Challenge｜开口练习

1. **What are you picking up for dinner?**
2. **Do you usually cook for yourself or prefer eating out?** 用 *I used to…, but now…* 回答。
3. **What's your go-to meal when you're busy?** 再用 *What about you?* 反问对方。

## 6. Listening｜拓展听力

可在 [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish) 检索 **Real Easy English** 的 cooking 相关话题。本篇对话为原创。

**Takeaway：** *Nothing fancy. / What's your go-to meal? / I don't feel like cooking. / What changed?*
