# 🎧 Daily Spoken English · Day 14
**Saturday, October 24, 2026 | 10–15 分钟 | A2–B2**

## 🏠 Viewing an Apartment → Clarifying Rent and Lease Terms
**今日双话题：看房、判断是否合适 → 自然追问租金、押金和租约条款。**

今天的目标不是背一堆房地产术语，而是练习在看房时**主动提问、抓住对方回答里的细节继续追问，并在决定前确认费用**。对话中的金额和规则都是虚构情境，不代表德国实际租房政策。

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话
*Situation: Jordan is viewing an apartment. Taylor is showing the place.*

### Topic A — Viewing the Apartment
**1. Taylor:** Hey, thanks for coming by. Was the place easy to find?  
**Jordan:** Yeah, pretty easy. I like how close it is to the bus stop.

**2. Taylor:** That's one of the nice things about this area. Want to take a look around?  
**Jordan:** Sure. Is this the main living room?

**3. Taylor:** Yep. It gets a lot of sunlight in the afternoon.  
**Jordan:** Nice! Does it get noisy with the street right outside?

**4. Taylor:** Not too bad, especially with the windows closed.  
**Jordan:** Good to know. And is the apartment furnished?

**5. Taylor:** Just the kitchen. You'd need to bring your own furniture for the other rooms.  
**Jordan:** Got it. I could work with that. What about the rent?

### 🔄 自然转场
Jordan 先注意到**交通方便**，于是看房；接着根据采光、噪音、家具逐步判断是否适合自己。听到“只有厨房带家具”，Jordan 回应 **I could work with that**（这个条件我可以接受），再追问 **What about the rent?**（那租金呢？）。这不是突然换话题，而是从“住起来怎么样”自然进入“付得起吗”。

### Topic B — Rent, Deposit, and Lease Terms
**6. Taylor:** It's 850 euros a month.  
**Jordan:** Okay. Does that include utilities, or are they extra?

**7. Taylor:** Heating and water are included, but electricity and internet are separate.  
**Jordan:** Thanks for clarifying. Roughly how much should I budget for those?

**8. Taylor:** It depends on how much you use. I can send you an estimate later.  
**Jordan:** That'd help. And how much is the security deposit?

**9. Taylor:** It's 1,700 euros for this place.  
**Jordan:** I see. Would I need to pay that up front?

**10. Taylor:** We can go over the payment details before you sign anything.  
**Jordan:** Sounds good. How long is the lease for?

**11. Taylor:** The proposed lease is for twelve months. When would you want to move in?  
**Jordan:** Ideally at the start of next month. Is that possible?

**12. Taylor:** It might be. I'll check and get back to you tomorrow.  
**Jordan:** Perfect, thanks. Could you also send me the lease so I can read through it?

## ② Understand the Conversation｜重点难句

**1. Does it get noisy with the street right outside?**  
“外面紧挨着马路，会不会很吵？”这里的 **get + adjective** 表示变得或出现某种状态：*Does it get cold at night?*（晚上会变冷吗？）

**2. I could work with that.**  
“这个条件我可以接受／想办法适应。”不是“我能和它一起工作”。它通常表示方案不完美，但仍可行：*The room is small, but I could work with that.*

**3. Does that include utilities, or are they extra?**  
“租金包括水电等杂费吗，还是另外收费？”**utilities** 通常指水、电、燃气等公共事业费用；具体包括什么，要看合同，不能只凭这个词推断。

**4. Roughly how much should I budget for those?**  
“这些费用我大概要预留多少钱？”**roughly** 让你询问估算值，而不是要求对方给出精确数字。

**5. Would I need to pay that up front?**  
“这笔钱需要预先支付吗？”**up front** 指在某个阶段开始前或交易开始时付款；它不自动意味着现金支付。

**6. Could you send me the lease so I can read through it?**  
“能把租约发给我，让我仔细读一下吗？”**read through** 强调从头到尾认真看一遍，比 *read it* 更突出核对条款。

## ④ Reusable Patterns｜3 个万能句型

**A. Is … included, or is it extra?**  
“……包含在内，还是另外收费？”  
*Is internet included, or is it extra?*（网络费包含吗，还是另付？）

**B. Roughly how much should I budget for …?**  
“……我大概要预留多少钱？”  
*Roughly how much should I budget for transport?*（交通费大概要预算多少？）

**C. Could you … so I can …?**  
“你能……，这样我就能……吗？”  
*Could you send me the details so I can compare my options?*（你能把细节发给我，方便我比较选择吗？）

## ⑤ Pronunciation｜2 个自然语流技巧

**1. Does that include…?** /dʌz ðæt ɪnˈkluːd/  
*does* 常弱读，**that‿include** 会连起来。练习：*Does that include heating?* 重点重读 **include** 和 **heating**，不必刻意吞音。

**2. Get back to you** /ɡet bæk tə ju/  
这里 **to** 常弱读为 /tə/。*I'll get back to you tomorrow* 中重音落在 **back** 和 **tomorrow**，说起来会更自然。

**英美用词小区别：** 美国常说 *apartment*，英国常说 *flat*；美国常说 *lease*，英国日常也常说 *tenancy agreement*。在德国谈租房，问清实际合同条款比套用任何一种英语说法更重要。

## ⑥ Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

**Taylor:** Heating and water are included, but electricity is separate.  
**Jordan:** **Thanks for clarifying.**（React：回应信息）  
**Jordan:** **So electricity isn't included.**（Pick up：抓住“电费另付”）  
**Jordan:** **Roughly how much should I budget for it?**（Follow-up：自然追问）

一个实用原则：**不要每次都跳到下一个预设问题。** 听到 *separate* 就追问费用；听到 *twelve months* 就追问起租时间；听到 *I'll check* 就问什么时候回复。这样才像真实对话。

## ⑧ Speaking Challenge｜连续角色扮演

**Round 1 — 看房：**  
**Taylor:** *Welcome! What would you like to know about the apartment?*  
请询问采光或噪音，并根据我的回答继续追问一个细节。

**Round 2 — 问费用：**  
**Taylor:** *The rent is 850 euros, and some utilities are included.*  
请先回应，再问具体包含什么，以及其他费用大概要多少。

**Round 3 — 确认下一步：**

请询问押金或租期，最后礼貌要求对方发租约供你阅读。
