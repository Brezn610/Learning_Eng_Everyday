# Day 48 — Bringing Up Late-Night Noise with a Flatmate → Agreeing on Quiet Hours and Guest Plans

**今日话题：** 礼貌地和室友沟通深夜噪音 → 一起商量安静时段和朋友来访的安排

**练习时长：** 10–15 分钟 · **目标：** 不指责对方，也能清楚表达自己的困扰；通过回应、抓细节和追问，把一次抱怨变成双方都能接受的生活约定。

## Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Alex and Sam share an apartment. Alex has an early start and wants to talk to Sam about music playing late at night. The conversation moves naturally from noise to quiet hours and having friends over.*

### Topic A — Bringing Up Late-Night Noise

**1. Alex:** Hey, Sam, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk about something from last night.  
**Sam:** Sure. What's up?

**2. Alex:** I don't mind you playing music, but it was pretty loud after midnight. I had an early meeting, so I had a hard time falling asleep.  
**Sam:** Oh, I'm sorry. I didn't realize it was carrying through the wall.

**3. Alex:** No worries. I know you weren't trying to keep me up. I just wanted to bring it up before it becomes a regular thing.  
**Sam:** That's fair. I had a couple of friends over, and we didn't notice how late it had got.

**4. Alex:** I get that. Could you turn it down a bit later at night, especially on weekdays?  
**Sam:** Yeah, absolutely. If it's ever too loud, you can just let me know.

**5. Alex:** Thanks. I'd rather we agree on something beforehand, though, so neither of us has to guess what's okay.  
**Sam:** That makes sense. What kind of quiet hours would work for you?

**6. Alex:** Maybe after 10:30 on weeknights? I'm usually up early, but I'm not asking for complete silence. Just keep the music and voices down.  
**Sam:** I can do that. I might still watch something or talk with friends, but I'll use headphones or keep it low.

### Topic B — Agreeing on Quiet Hours and Guest Plans

**7. Alex:** That sounds good. And weekends can be a little more flexible, as long as we give each other a heads-up.  
**Sam:** Sure. I sometimes have people over on Friday nights. Would a quick message earlier in the day be enough?

**8. Alex:** Definitely. It helps to know in advance. If it's a bigger group, could you also let me know roughly how many people are coming?  
**Sam:** Of course. And if you have an early start the next morning, just tell me. We can wrap things up earlier.

**9. Alex:** Thanks, I appreciate that. I'll do the same if I invite anyone over.  
**Sam:** Great. Do you want to make a simple rule for the shared living room, too?

**10. Alex:** Maybe just leave it tidy after people leave. I don't mind having guests over; I just don't want to find cups and food on the table the next morning.  
**Sam:** Fair enough. I'll make sure we clean up before going to bed.

**11. Alex:** Perfect. So, weeknights after 10:30, keep the noise down; on weekends, give each other a heads-up about guests; and whoever hosts tidies up afterward.  
**Sam:** Sounds good. And if something doesn't work, let's just talk about it rather than let it build up.

**12. Alex:** Deal. Thanks for being cool about it. I know sharing a place means making a few adjustments.  
**Sam:** No problem. I'd rather we talk things through and keep the place comfortable for both of us.

### 自然转场为什么成立？

**Topic A：先谈深夜噪音。** Alex 没有直接说 “You're too noisy”，而是描述具体情况：音乐过了午夜仍然很响，自己第二天有早会，因此难以入睡。Sam 解释当晚有朋友来访，这个细节自然引出周末聚会。

**Topic B：从噪音延伸到共同生活约定。** 既然问题发生在朋友来访时，两人就进一步商量 quiet hours、提前通知（give each other a heads-up），以及聚会后的清理责任。

这段对话的重点不是“制定一堆规则”，而是用协商语气解决实际问题：先说明影响，再提出可执行的请求，也主动承诺自己会遵守相同约定。

### 重要难句

- **I wanted to bring it up before it becomes a regular thing.**  
  我想趁它还没变成经常发生的事，先提出来谈谈。
- **I'd rather we agree on something beforehand, so neither of us has to guess what's okay.**  
  我更希望我们提前商量好，这样彼此都不用猜什么是可以接受的。
- **I don't mind having guests over; I just don't want to find cups and food on the table the next morning.**  
  我不介意有人来做客，只是不希望第二天早上看到桌上还留着杯子和食物。
- **Let's talk about it rather than let it build up.**  
  我们有问题就谈，不要让不满一直积累。

**特别记住：**

- **bring it up** 不等于争吵，只是主动提出一个话题或问题。
- **keep me up** 常用于噪音、压力或其他让人睡不着的情况。
- **give me a heads-up** 很口语，适合室友、朋友和同事之间提前打招呼。
- **have people over** 比 *invite people to my home* 更自然、更日常。

## Reusable Patterns｜3 个可复用句型

### 1. I don't mind..., but...

先表示自己并不反对某件事，再说明具体困扰。

- I don't mind you cooking late, but could you keep the noise down?
- I don't mind having guests over, but I'd like a little notice.
- I don't mind sharing the kitchen, but let's clean up afterward.

### 2. Could we agree on...?

提出共同约定，而不是单方面命令。

- Could we agree on quiet hours during the week?
- Could we agree on how to split the cleaning?
- Could we agree to give each other a heads-up before inviting guests?

### 3. I'd rather we..., so...

表达自己更倾向的解决办法，并说明原因。

- I'd rather we talk about it now, so it doesn't become a bigger issue.
- I'd rather we plan ahead, so nobody gets caught off guard.
- I'd rather we keep the rules simple, so they're easy to follow.

## Pronunciation｜自然美式语流

**1. Could you**

在自然美式口语中，*could you* 常发生连读和音变，听起来会接近一个紧凑的音节组合。不要把两个词分开、平均用力：

- Could you turn it down a little?
- Could you give me a heads-up?
- Could you keep it low after ten?

把主要重音放在 **turn down、heads-up、low** 等信息词上。

**2. I'd rather we...**

*I'd* 和后面的 *rather* 通常连得很紧；*we* 不需要特别重读，除非你想强调“我们双方”。练习时把重音放在真正的重点上：

- I'd rather we agree on something.
- I'd rather we talk things through.
- I'd rather we keep it simple.

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设室友说：

**Sam:** I sometimes have people over on Friday nights. Would a quick message earlier in the day be enough?

**1 · React**

That works for me.

这样我可以接受。

**2 · Pick up a detail**

So Friday nights are usually when you have friends over.

所以你通常会在周五晚上邀请朋友来。

**3 · Ask a follow-up**

Could you give me a heads-up if it's going to be a bigger group?

如果人数比较多，你能提前告诉我一声吗？

再练一个：

**Sam:** I can keep the music low after 10:30.

- **React:** I appreciate that.
- **Pick up a detail:** So the main thing is keeping the volume down, not avoiding music altogether.
- **Ask a follow-up:** Would headphones work if you're watching something late?

不要只回答 “Okay”。接住对方的具体信息，再问一个能帮助双方把安排说清楚的问题。

## Speaking Challenge｜3 轮连续角色扮演

你扮演 Alex，我扮演 Sam（flatmate）。每轮尽量回答 3–5 句话。不要只完成任务清单，要回应我上一句话里的一个细节，再自然追问。

### Round 1 — Bringing Up the Noise

**Sam:** Hey, you seem a bit tired today. Everything okay?

你的任务：
- 说明昨晚音乐有点大，影响了睡眠。
- 表达你知道对方可能不是故意的。
- 礼貌地请求晚上把音量调低。

### Round 2 — Agreeing on Quiet Hours

**Sam:** Sorry about that. I sometimes lose track of time when friends are over. What would work for you?

你的任务：
- 提议工作日晚上 10:30 以后保持安静。
- 说明你不是要求完全不能说话或听音乐。
- 追问周末能否灵活一些，但提前互相通知。

### Round 3 — Making a Shared Agreement

**Sam:** That sounds fair. I can text you before having people over. Is there anything else we should agree on?

你的任务：
- 确认朋友来访时如何提前告知。
- 提出聚会后整理公共区域。
- 用 **So, just to make sure we're on the same page...** 总结双方约定。
- 主动补充自己也会遵守相同规则。
