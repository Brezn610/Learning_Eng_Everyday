# 🎧 Daily Spoken English · Day 18
**Wednesday, October 28, 2026**

## 🤝 Starting a Conversation at a Tech Event → Staying in Touch

**今日双话题：在技术活动上自然搭话 → 从共同兴趣聊到实践机会，并保持联系**

⏱️ **10–15 分钟**｜A2–B2｜🇺🇸 自然美式口语

今天练习在技术展会、讲座或职业活动中与陌生人交流。重点不是一上来就问有没有工作，而是**先聊对方展示的东西，抓住一个细节追问，发现共同兴趣，再自然地提出保持联系**。

**Today's goal:** Start a conversation, ask thoughtful follow-up questions, and make a genuine connection.

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创双人对话

*Situation: Riley, a student, meets Morgan, an engineer, after a short AI demo at a networking event. 人物与项目均为虚构。*

### Topic A — Starting a Conversation at a Tech Event

**1. Riley:** Hey, I caught your demo earlier. That was really interesting.  
**Morgan:** Thanks! What stood out to you?

**2. Riley:** The part where the system handled messy input. Does that happen often?  
**Morgan:** All the time. Real-world data is rarely clean.

**3. Riley:** I can imagine. How do you test it before real users try it?  
**Morgan:** We start with a small pilot and track how often things go wrong.

**4. Riley:** So you look beyond whether the demo works?  
**Morgan:** Exactly. A good demo is one thing; a reliable product is another.

**5. Riley:** That makes sense. What's been the biggest challenge so far?  
**Morgan:** Honestly, getting useful feedback from real users.

**6. Riley:** Interesting. Do students ever get involved in that kind of work?  
**Morgan:** Actually, yes. We had an intern help with our last pilot.

### 🔄 Natural Transition｜转场逻辑

Riley 先对 **demo** 表达真实兴趣，再追问 **messy input → testing → real users**。Morgan 提到真实用户反馈后，Riley 问学生是否能参与；Morgan 自然说到一位 **intern**。于是话题从技术讨论转向实践经验和建立联系，不需要生硬地说 *Speaking of internships…*。

### Topic B — Turning a Good Conversation into a Connection

**7. Riley:** Oh, nice. What did they work on?  
**Morgan:** Mostly testing and gathering feedback, with some coding too.

**8. Riley:** That's the kind of hands-on experience I'm looking for. How did they get started?  
**Morgan:** They reached out with a small project and explained what they'd learned.

**9. Riley:** Good to know. Would it be okay if I sent you a short summary of my project?  
**Morgan:** Sure. Keep it brief and focus on the problem you solved.

**10. Riley:** Got it. Is LinkedIn the best way to stay in touch?  
**Morgan:** Yeah, send me a connection request and mention this conversation.

**11. Riley:** Will do. Is there one skill you'd recommend I work on?  
**Morgan:** Explaining your results clearly, not just showing the code.

**12. Riley:** That's really helpful. Thanks for taking the time.  
**Morgan:** Of course. Good luck, and I look forward to hearing from you.

### 重要难句

**What stood out to you?** — 哪一点最让你印象深刻？这里的 *stand out* 是“突出、引人注意”，不是站到外面。

**A good demo is one thing; a reliable product is another.** — 演示能跑通是一回事，产品能可靠运行是另一回事。*X is one thing; Y is another* 用来区分两种不同的要求。

**Would it be okay if I sent you a short summary?** — 我之后发一份简短介绍给你，可以吗？比直接说 *Give me your contact* 自然、礼貌。

## ③ Reusable Patterns｜三个万能句型

**A. What stood out to you about …?** — 询问对方最关注的细节。

*What stood out to you about the talk?*  
这场讲座有什么让你印象深刻？

**B. How did you get started with …?** — 询问经历，容易引出故事。

*How did you get started with robotics?*  
你最初是怎么接触机器人领域的？

**C. Would it be okay if I …?** — 礼貌提出请求。

*Would it be okay if I connected with you on LinkedIn?*  
我可以在 LinkedIn 上加你吗？

这里的 *if I connected* 使用过去式来表达更委婉的假设，不表示过去发生过。

## ④ Pronunciation｜两个自然美式发音技巧

**1. Did you → /ˈdɪdʒə/**

*Did you enjoy the talk?*  
自然语速中 /d/ 与 /j/ 常融合成 /dʒ/。先读清楚，再逐渐连读，不需要刻意吞音。

**2. Would it be → /wʊd ɪt bi/**

*Would it be okay if I sent you a message?*  
在美式英语中，*would it* 连接时 /d/ 可以自然衔接后面的元音；重音通常落在 **okay** 和 **message**，而不是每个单词都一样重。

**🇺🇸 / 🇬🇧 小区别：** 美国求职常说 *résumé*，英国更常说 *CV*；*internship* 两边都能理解，英国也常见 *work placement*，但具体形式不完全一样。

## ⑤ Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设对方说：

**Morgan:** We ran a small pilot with real users.

你可以回答：

**Riley:** Oh, that's interesting. So you've already tested it with real people. What kind of feedback did you get?

**React：** *Oh, that's interesting.* — 先表示兴趣。

**Pick up a detail：** *You've already tested it with real people.* — 抓住 *pilot / real users*。

**Ask a follow-up：** *What kind of feedback did you get?* — 用开放式问题让对方多说。

如果对方回答 *Users found it confusing*，就继续问：*What part confused them?* 这比突然转到“你们招人吗”更自然，也更容易建立真正的交流。

## ⑦ Speaking Challenge｜三轮连续角色扮演

**Round 1 — Break the ice**  
**Morgan:** *Hey, did you get a chance to see the demo? What did you think?*  
先回应，再说一个具体亮点，最后问一个技术细节。

**Round 2 — Explore a shared interest**  
**Morgan:** *We've been testing it with real users, but it's still early.*  
使用 *React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up*，问他们怎么测试、遇到了什么困难，再简短分享自己的相关兴趣。

**Round 3 — Keep the connection going**  
**Morgan:** *Sounds like you're interested in this kind of work.*  
用一句话说明你想获得什么实践经验；礼貌询问是否能在 LinkedIn 联系，并问对方推荐你提升哪项技能。

尝试三轮连起来说 **3–5 分钟**。最后再反问一句，让对话自然结束，而不是只说 *Thank you*。
