# 🎧 Daily Spoken English · Day 23 — Calling In Sick → Handing Off Urgent Work

**Monday, November 2, 2026 · 10–15 分钟 · A2–B2 · 美式自然口语**

**今日双话题：** 身体不舒服，向主管请病假 → 发现今天有一项紧急任务，简短交接后安心休息。

**Today's goal:** Explain that you need a sick day, identify one urgent task, and agree on a simple handoff without overexplaining.

> 对话纯属虚构，是英语口语练习。请病假的具体通知、证明与薪资规则应以实际单位和当地要求为准。

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创双人对话

*Situation: Jamie calls their manager Morgan on Monday morning.*

### Topic A — Calling In Sick

**1. Jamie:** Hi, Morgan. It's Jamie. Do you have a minute?  
**Morgan:** Of course. What's up?

**2. Jamie:** I'm not feeling well today. I woke up with a fever and a sore throat.  
**Morgan:** Sorry to hear that. Do you need to take the day off?

**3. Jamie:** I think so. I'm really not up to working today.  
**Morgan:** No problem. Please rest and follow our usual sick-leave procedure.

**4. Jamie:** Thanks. Should I let the rest of the team know?  
**Morgan:** I'll tell them you're out today. You don't need to share medical details.

**5. Jamie:** I appreciate it. I'm just worried about the client update this afternoon.  
**Morgan:** Oh, right. Is there anything time-sensitive we need to cover?

**6. Jamie:** Just one thing. The draft is ready, but it hasn't been sent.  
**Morgan:** Got it. Where can I find it?

### 🔄 自然转场

Jamie 先说明今天无法工作。Morgan 接受请假并表示会通知团队。随后 Jamie 提到 **the client update this afternoon**（今天下午的客户进度更新）。Morgan 抓住 **time-sensitive**（有时效要求）这个细节追问，于是从 Topic A「请病假」自然转到 Topic B「紧急任务交接」。这不是要求生病的人继续工作，而是用最少的信息明确谁接手。

### Topic B — Handing Off Urgent Work

**7. Jamie:** It's in the shared project folder, under “Client Update.”  
**Morgan:** Perfect. Is there anything I should double-check before sending it?

**8. Jamie:** The numbers on the last slide. They're from yesterday's report.  
**Morgan:** Thanks for the heads-up. I'll compare them with the latest report.

**9. Jamie:** Great. Could Alex handle the final review?  
**Morgan:** Sure. I'll ask Alex to take over.

**10. Jamie:** That takes a load off my mind.  
**Morgan:** Glad to help. Anything else that can't wait?

**11. Jamie:** No, everything else can wait until I'm back.  
**Morgan:** Good. Don't worry about checking messages today.

**12. Jamie:** Thanks, Morgan. I'll update you when I know whether I can come in tomorrow.  
**Morgan:** Sounds good. Feel better soon!

### 重要难句

**I'm really not up to working today.** — 我今天确实没有精力工作。**be up to + 名词 / -ing** 可以表示有能力、有精力做某事。

**Is there anything time-sensitive we need to cover?** — 有没有什么必须及时处理、需要我们接手的事？**cover** 在这里是处理、代为照看。

**Thanks for the heads-up.** — 谢谢提前提醒。常用于别人告诉你一个可能影响计划的重要细节。

**That takes a load off my mind.** — 这让我放心多了。表示原先担心的事得到解决。

**特别注意：** **call in sick** 是“通知单位自己生病不能来”；**be sick of something** 是“厌烦某事”，意思完全不同。例如 *I'm sick of these delays* 是“这些延误让我烦透了”，不是“我因为延误而生病”。

## ③ Reusable Patterns｜三个高频句型

**A. I'm not feeling well, so I need to…** — 简洁说明情况与需要。

*I'm not feeling well, so I need to take the day off.*  
我身体不舒服，所以今天需要请假。

**B. Is there anything … we need to …?** — 询问是否有需要优先处理的事。

*Is there anything urgent we need to finish today?*  
今天有没有什么紧急事情必须完成？

**C. Could + 人 + 动词原形 …?** — 礼貌提出交接请求。

*Could someone else handle the final review?*  
能让其他人负责最后检查吗？

不需要为了显得礼貌而解释所有健康细节。明确说明无法到岗、遵循单位流程，并在必要时提供简短交接即可。

## ④ Pronunciation｜两个美式自然语流技巧

**1. not up to 的连接与闪音**

*I'm not up to working today.*

在自然美式口语里，**not up** 的 /t/ 在元音之间可能接近闪音 [ɾ]，听起来类似 /nɑɾ ʌp/；**to** 往往弱读 /tə/。先清楚读，再连贯读。

**2. I'll ask 的辅音与元音连接**

*I'll ask Alex to take over.*

**I'll ask** /aɪl æsk/ 读起来可以连成一个流畅的音组，重点落在 **ask Alex** 与 **take over** 上。

**英美小区别：** 美式常说 *call in sick* 或 *take a sick day*；英式也说 *phone in sick*、*be off sick*。这些表达都很常见，具体请假制度不能仅凭词语推断。

## ⑤ Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

**Jamie:** The draft is ready, but it hasn't been sent.

**Morgan:** Got it. So the draft's finished, but someone still needs to send it. Is there anything we should check first?

**React:** *Got it.* — 明白了。  
**Pick up a detail:** *The draft's finished, but someone still needs to send it.* — 抓住“写好了，但尚未发送”。  
**Ask a follow-up:** *Is there anything we should check first?* — 继续追问具体风险。

如果对方回答 *The numbers might be outdated*，就自然接：*Good catch. Where can I find the latest figures?*（提醒得好。最新数据在哪里？）

这比突然问另一项不相关的工作更自然。**重点是问清一个具体细节，然后把责任交给合适的人。**

## ⑦ Speaking Challenge｜连续三轮角色扮演

**Round 1 — Call in sick**  
**Morgan:** *Hi, Jamie. What's up?*  
用两句话说明你今天不舒服、需要请假。尝试使用 *I'm not feeling well…*，并礼貌确认接下来需要做什么。

**Round 2 — Mention the urgent task**  
**Morgan:** *No problem. Is there anything urgent we should know about?*  
先回应，再提到一项今天必须处理的任务，说明文件在哪里、什么地方需要检查。

**Round 3 — Confirm the handoff**  
**Morgan:** *Okay, I'll ask someone to take over. Anything else?*  
表示感谢，确认其余工作可以等到你回来再处理，并说明什么时候更新自己的到岗情况。

把三轮连成 3–5 分钟的对话。即使对方是主管，也可以主动反问：*Is there anything else you need from me before I sign off?*
