# Day 28 — Making Weekend Plans → Adjusting Plans When the Weather Changes

**今日话题：** 约朋友周末野餐 → 发现天气可能变化后，商量替代方案。

**Today's goal:** Make a casual invitation, agree on practical details, react to a change, and suggest an alternative without letting the conversation die.

## Real Conversation｜12 轮原创双人对话

*Situation: Maya and Leo are friends planning to meet on Saturday.*

### Topic A — Making Weekend Plans

1. **Maya:** Are you still up for a picnic on Saturday?  
   **Leo:** Yeah, sounds fun. What time were you thinking?

2. **Maya:** Maybe around noon, before the park gets too crowded.  
   **Leo:** Works for me. Should we bring our own food?

3. **Maya:** I can make some sandwiches. Could you bring something to drink?  
   **Leo:** Sure. Any preferences?

4. **Maya:** Water is fine. Maybe bring some fruit too, if you don't mind.  
   **Leo:** No problem. Should we meet at the main entrance?

5. **Maya:** Yeah, near the big fountain. It's easy to find.  
   **Leo:** Great. I'll check the weather before we head out.

6. **Maya:** Good idea. It might be a little chilly, so I'll bring a jacket.  
   **Leo:** Actually, I just checked the forecast. It looks like rain on Saturday afternoon.

### 自然转场：从约定细节到调整计划

前面两个人已经商量好时间、食物和集合地点。Leo 主动提到自己会查看天气，随后发现周六下午可能下雨。这个新信息直接影响野餐是否可行，于是话题自然转向：雨会下多久、是否提前出门，以及要不要换成室内活动。

### Topic B — Adjusting Plans When the Weather Changes

7. **Maya:** Oh, really? Is it supposed to rain all day, or just later?  
   **Leo:** Just after two, according to the forecast.

8. **Maya:** We could still go earlier, but the grass might be wet.  
   **Leo:** True. I'd rather not sit on wet ground.

9. **Maya:** Do you want to switch to something indoors?  
   **Leo:** That could work. There's a new food hall downtown.

10. **Maya:** Oh, I've heard about that place. Is it easy to get a table?  
    **Leo:** It can get busy, but we could go right around noon.

11. **Maya:** I'm in. Let's keep the same time and meet there instead.  
    **Leo:** Perfect. We can grab lunch and walk around afterward if the weather clears up.

12. **Maya:** Sounds good. I'll message you if anything changes.  
    **Leo:** Great. And I'll keep you posted if the forecast changes again.

## 重要表达｜中文理解

- **Are you still up for a picnic?** 你周六还想去野餐吗？up for 表示“有兴趣／愿意做某事”，很适合朋友间邀请。
- **What time were you thinking?** 你想的是几点？比直接问 What time? 更柔和、更自然。
- **It looks like rain.** 看起来要下雨了。这里是在根据迹象作判断。
- **Is it supposed to rain all day?** 预计会下一整天吗？supposed to 在这里表示天气预报或安排中的预期。
- **I'd rather not sit on wet ground.** 我还是不太想坐在湿地上。I'd rather not... 是礼貌表达偏好的方式。
- **I'll keep you posted.** 有新情况我会及时告诉你。

## Reusable Patterns｜三个可复用句型

### 1. Are you still up for ...?
用于轻松确认对方是否还想参加原定活动。

- Are you still up for coffee tomorrow?
- Are you still up for the hike this weekend?

### 2. Do you want to switch to ...?
当原计划不太合适时，提出替代方案。

- Do you want to switch to an indoor activity?
- We could switch to Sunday if Saturday gets too busy.

### 3. We could ..., if ...
提出一个有弹性的备选方案。

- We could meet earlier if the rain starts in the afternoon.
- We could get a table inside if it's too cold outside.

## Pronunciation｜两个自然美式发音技巧

1. **Could you → /kʊdʒə/**：在自然语流中，could you 常连读并发生音变，听起来接近 “couldja”。练习：Could you bring something to drink? 不要把两个词完全分开、逐字重读。

2. **A little → /ə ˈlɪɾəl/**：美式英语中，little 的 /t/ 常变成轻快的闪音 [ɾ]，听起来有点像很轻的 d。练习：It might be a little chilly. 重点是自然连贯，不要刻意夸张闪音。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设朋友说：

**Leo:** It looks like rain on Saturday afternoon.

- **React:** Oh, that's a shame.  
  哦，那有点可惜。
- **Pick up a detail:** So the rain starts later in the afternoon?  
  所以雨是下午晚些时候才开始？
- **Ask a follow-up:** Could we go a bit earlier, or would you rather do something indoors?  
  我们能不能早点去，还是你更想安排室内活动？

把三步连起来：

**Oh, that's a shame. So the rain starts later in the afternoon? Could we go a bit earlier, or would you rather do something indoors?**

如果对方说雨可能只下一小会儿，可以继续问：

**Would it be worth bringing a picnic blanket anyway?**  
那我们还是带条野餐垫比较好吗？

重点：先回应对方的消息，再抓住“下雨时间”这个细节，最后提出一个真正有帮助的追问。不要为了延长对话而突然换话题。

## Speaking Challenge｜三个连续角色扮演问题

**Round 1 — Make the invitation**  
**Leo:** Are you free this Saturday? I was thinking about doing something outdoors.

用 3–4 句话邀请对方野餐或散步，提出时间和地点，并问对方是否有空。

**Round 2 — React to the weather**  
**Leo:** I just checked the forecast. It might rain after two.

先回应，再确认雨大概什么时候开始，接着讨论是否提前出发。

**Round 3 — Agree on a backup plan**  
**Leo:** We could go to an indoor food hall instead. What do you think?

比较原计划和替代方案，说明自己的偏好，最后确认时间和集合地点。至少主动问对方一个 follow-up question。
