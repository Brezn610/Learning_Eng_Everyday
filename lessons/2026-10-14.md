# 🎧 Daily Spoken English · Day 04

**Wednesday, October 14, 2026**

## 🤒 Not Feeling Well → Booking a Doctor's Appointment
**今日双话题：描述身体不舒服 → 预约医生**

⏱️ **10–15 分钟** · **A2–B1+** · 🇺🇸 自然美式英语

**Situation：** 你在学校遇见同学。对方发现你精神不太好，从关心身体状况，自然聊到要不要预约医生、如何描述症状。

**今日目标：** 不需要背复杂医学词汇，只要能说清楚「哪里不舒服、持续多久、想预约什么时间」。

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

**Topic A — Not Feeling Well**

1. **Alex:** Hey, you look a little tired. Everything okay?  
   **You:** Not really. I've had a sore throat since Monday.
2. **Alex:** Oh no. Do you have a fever?  
   **You:** I don't think so, but I've been feeling pretty run-down.
3. **Alex:** That sounds rough. Have you been able to get some rest?  
   **You:** Not much. I've been busy with assignments.
4. **Alex:** I see. Is it getting any better?  
   **You:** A little, but my throat still hurts when I swallow.
5. **Alex:** Maybe you should see a doctor if it doesn't get better.  
   **You:** Yeah, I've been thinking about it.
6. **Alex:** Have you made an appointment yet?  
   **You:** Not yet. I'm not sure what to say when I call.

**Topic B — Booking an Appointment**

7. **Alex:** You can just say, “I'd like to make an appointment.”  
   **You:** Okay. How do I ask about the time?
8. **Alex:** Try, “Do you have anything available this week?”  
   **You:** That's simple enough. Will they ask about my symptoms?
9. **Alex:** Probably. Just tell them when it started and how you're feeling.  
   **You:** So I could say, “I've had a sore throat for a few days”?
10. **Alex:** Exactly. And if you need to miss class, you can ask about a doctor's note.  
    **You:** Good point. I might need to take a day off.
11. **Alex:** Hopefully you'll feel better soon.  
    **You:** Thanks. I'll give the doctor's office a call after lunch.
12. **Alex:** Sounds good. Take care of yourself!  
    **You:** Thanks, I will. See you later!

**转场怎么发生的？** 第 4–6 轮从“症状有没有改善”→“要不要看医生”→“预约了没有”；不是硬换话题。

**三个难句：**
- **I've been feeling pretty run-down.** 最近一直觉得疲惫、没精神。
- **Is it getting any better?** 情况有好转吗？
- **Do you have anything available this week?** 这周还有可以预约的时间吗？这里 *anything* 指预约时段。

### ⭐ 三个万能句型

**A. I've had … since / for …** — 我……已经持续……了。
- *I've had a sore throat since Monday.*（从周一开始）
- *I've had a headache for two days.*（持续两天）

**B. Do you have anything available …?** — ……有可以预约的时间吗？
- *Do you have anything available tomorrow morning?*
- *Do you have anything available this week?*

**C. I'm thinking of + -ing** — 我正考虑……
- *I'm thinking of seeing a doctor.*
- *I'm thinking of taking a day off.*

## ③ Pronunciation｜发音训练

**1. Have you been…?**  
*Have you been able to rest?*  
自然口语里，*have* 和 *you* 会连接，*you* 常弱读为 /jə/；*been* 在美式英语里常读 /bɪn/。先清晰读：/hæv ju bɪn/，再练习连贯的 /həv jə bɪn/。不必故意吞音。

**2. I'd like to…**  
*I'd like to make an appointment.*  
*to* 在句中通常弱读 /tə/，整句可练为 *I'd like tə make an appointment.* 重点是让对方听清楚 *appointment*。

**英美区别：** 美国常说 *doctor's office*，英国常说 *GP surgery* 或 *doctor's surgery*；美国 *doctor's note*，英国常说 *sick note*（正式证明也称 *fit note*）。

## ④ Conversation Skill｜先回应，再追问

**React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up**

**A:** *I've been feeling sick since Monday.*  
**B:** *Oh no, that sounds rough. Is it getting any better?*

这里 **That sounds rough** 是对对方状态的回应；**Is it getting any better?** 则抓住“持续生病”这一细节继续追问。

如果对方说 *Not really*，你可以自然继续：*Have you thought about seeing a doctor?*

## ⑥ Speaking Challenge｜连续角色扮演

假设你打电话给一家诊所，我来扮演接待员。请尝试**连续回答并主动追问**：

1. **Receptionist:** *Good morning. How can I help you?*  
   目标：说出想预约医生。
2. **Receptionist:** *Of course. Could you briefly tell me what's wrong?*  
   目标：描述症状和持续时间，使用 *I've had … for / since …*。
3. **Receptionist:** *We have an opening on Friday afternoon. Would that work for you?*  
   目标：接受或询问更早的时间，最后说一句自然的感谢。

**Today's takeaway：** *I've had … since … / Is it getting any better? / I'd like to make an appointment. / Do you have anything available …?*

> 语言练习场景，不是医疗诊断；真实症状持续、加重或严重时应寻求适当医疗帮助。
