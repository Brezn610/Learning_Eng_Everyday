# 🎧 Day 26 — Reporting a Heating Problem → Arranging a Repair Visit

**今日双话题：** 向物业说明暖气不工作 → 根据维修人员的时间安排上门检修。  
**目标：** 清楚描述问题、回答简单排查问题、询问维修安排，并确认上门时间。  
**建议用时：** 10–15 分钟。人物与情境均为虚构。

**Today's goal:** Explain a household problem, answer a few troubleshooting questions, arrange a repair visit, and confirm the details.

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Morgan calls the building manager because one radiator in the apartment isn't heating up.*

### Topic A — Reporting a Heating Problem

1. **Morgan:** Hi, I'm calling about the heating in my apartment. It's not coming on properly.  
   **Casey:** Sorry to hear that. Is it happening in every room or just one?

2. **Morgan:** Just the bedroom. The radiator has been cold since last night.  
   **Casey:** Okay. Is the heating working in the living room?

3. **Morgan:** Yeah, the living room is warm. It's just this one radiator.  
   **Casey:** Got it. Have you tried turning the thermostat up?

4. **Morgan:** I have. I set it to 22, but the bedroom is still cold.  
   **Casey:** Thanks for checking. Sometimes the radiator valve can be the issue.

5. **Morgan:** I'm not sure which part that is. I don't want to mess with it.  
   **Casey:** That's totally fine. You don't need to take anything apart.

6. **Morgan:** Okay, good. Is there anything else I should check?  
   **Casey:** No, that's enough for now. We can send a technician to take a look.

### 自然转场为什么成立？

Morgan 原本是在报告暖气故障。物业先确认问题发生在哪个房间，又问了温控器是否调高。Morgan 不确定如何处理阀门，也不想自己拆动设备。

Casey 因此提出 **send a technician to take a look**。对话的重点自然从“描述和排查故障”转向“安排维修人员上门”，进入 Topic B。

### Topic B — Arranging a Repair Visit

7. **Morgan:** That would be great. Would someone be able to come tomorrow?  
   **Casey:** Let me check. We have a technician available tomorrow afternoon.

8. **Morgan:** I have class until 3:30. Could they come sometime after four?  
   **Casey:** I think so. We can offer a time window between four and six.

9. **Morgan:** That works for me. Do I need to be home the whole time?  
   **Casey:** Ideally, yes. The technician will need someone to let them in.

10. **Morgan:** No problem. I'll be home. Should I move anything before they arrive?  
    **Casey:** Just make sure there's some space around the radiator.

11. **Morgan:** Got it. So, tomorrow between four and six, and I should keep the area clear.  
    **Casey:** Exactly. I'll send you a confirmation with the details.

12. **Morgan:** Perfect. Could you let me know if the time changes?  
    **Casey:** Of course. We'll contact you if anything comes up.

### 难句精讲

- **I'm calling about the heating in my apartment.** 我打电话是想说一下公寓暖气的问题。*I'm calling about…* 是电话沟通中非常自然的开场。
- **The radiator has been cold since last night.** 暖气片从昨晚开始就是凉的。*has been … since…* 表示某种状态从过去持续到现在。
- **I don't want to mess with it.** 我不想乱动它／自己折腾它。*mess with* 在这里表示动手摆弄，语气很日常。
- **We can send a technician to take a look.** 我们可以派维修人员来看一下。*take a look* 比较口语，表示检查一下。
- **We can offer a time window between four and six.** 我们可以安排一个下午四点到六点之间的上门时间段。*time window* 指一个时间范围，不是精确到某一分钟。
- **I'll let you know if anything comes up.** 如果有什么变化，我会通知你。*come up* 在这里表示出现意外情况或新情况。

## ③ Reusable Patterns｜三个高频句型

### A. I'm calling about …

我打电话是想咨询／说明……

- *I'm calling about a problem with the heating.*  
  我打电话是想说一下暖气的问题。
- *I'm calling about the appointment I booked.*  
  我打电话是想问一下之前预约的事情。

### B. Would someone be able to …?

能不能安排人……

- *Would someone be able to come and check it?*  
  能安排人过来检查一下吗？
- *Would someone be able to call me back?*  
  能安排人给我回个电话吗？

### C. Would [time] work for you?

……这个时间你方便吗？

- *Would tomorrow afternoon work for you?*  
  明天下午你方便吗？
- *Would sometime after four work?*  
  四点以后某个时间可以吗？

## ④ Pronunciation｜两个自然美式发音技巧

### 1. Would you → /wʊdʒə/

在自然美式口语中，*would you* 常连成接近 **/wʊdʒə/** 的声音。

练习：

- *Would you be able to come tomorrow?*
- 先慢读 /wʊd juː/，再自然连读 /wʊdʒə/。

### 2. Let me → /ˈlɛɾmi/

美式英语中，*let me* 的 /t/ 在自然语流里可能变成闪音 [ɾ]，听起来接近 **“led-mee”** 或轻快的 “lemme”。

练习：

- *Let me check.*
- *Let me know if the time changes.*

先保证清楚，再练自然速度；不要为了连读而把词吞掉。

**英美小区别：** 美国日常常说 *the heat isn't working* 或 *the heat isn't coming on*；英国也常说 *the heating isn't working*。在美国，*heater* 也可能指独立的取暖设备，而 *heating* 常指整套供暖系统。

## ⑤ Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

假设物业说：

**Casey:** We can send a technician tomorrow afternoon, but I can't give you an exact time yet.

可以这样接：

**Morgan:** *Okay, so it'll be sometime in the afternoon. Do you have a rough time window? I need to plan around my class.*

- **React：** *Okay, so…* — 先表示理解。
- **Pick up a detail：** *It'll be sometime in the afternoon.* — 抓住“下午，但没有准确时间”这个细节。
- **Ask a follow-up：** *Do you have a rough time window?* — 进一步询问大概时间段。

如果对方说 *The technician will need access to the bedroom*，可以继续问：

*Sure. Is there anything I should move or prepare before they arrive?*

没问题。维修人员来之前，我需要移走或准备什么吗？

重点不是连续抛出很多问题，而是先回应，再抓住对方刚刚说的一个细节，最后提出一个真正有用的追问。

## ⑦ Speaking Challenge｜三轮连续角色扮演

### Round 1 — Report the problem

**Casey:** *Hi, building management. How can I help you?*

告诉对方暖气出了什么问题、从什么时候开始、影响哪个房间。再补充你已经尝试过什么。

### Round 2 — Arrange a visit

**Casey:** *We can send someone tomorrow afternoon. Would that work for you?*

先回应，再说明自己的课程或工作安排，提出一个合适的时间段。接着问对方是否能确认大概时间。

### Round 3 — Confirm the details

**Casey:** *The technician will come between four and six. Someone needs to let them in.*

确认自己会在家、询问是否需要提前准备，并用自己的话复述一次预约信息。最后问：

*Could you let me know if anything changes?*
