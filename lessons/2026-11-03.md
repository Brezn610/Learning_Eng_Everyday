# 🎧 Day 24 — Asking for Help in a Supermarket → Choosing an Alternative

**今日双话题：** 在超市找不到商品 → 发现缺货后询问替代品、价格和库存。**10–15 分钟｜A2–B2｜自然美式口语**

**Today's goal:** Ask for help, react to an unexpected answer, compare options, and decide what to buy.

## ① Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Chris is shopping for ingredients to make dinner. Taylor works at the supermarket. 人物和商品均为虚构。*

### Topic A — Asking for Help in a Supermarket

1. **Chris:** Excuse me, could you help me find the rice noodles?  
   **Taylor:** Sure! Have you checked the international food aisle?
2. **Chris:** I looked there, but I couldn't spot them.  
   **Taylor:** No problem. What kind are you looking for?
3. **Chris:** The thin ones you use for stir-fries.  
   **Taylor:** Got it. They'd normally be on this shelf.
4. **Chris:** Oh, I see the label, but the shelf is empty.  
   **Taylor:** Yeah, looks like we're out of stock.
5. **Chris:** Ah, that's a shame. Do you know when you'll get more?  
   **Taylor:** We might get a delivery Thursday, but I can't guarantee it.
6. **Chris:** I was hoping to cook tonight, so waiting won't really work.  
   **Taylor:** In that case, would you be open to trying a different kind?

**自然转场：** Chris 先询问商品位置，发现货架空了，又听说补货日期不确定。因为今晚就要做饭，Taylor 才顺着“等不了”这个细节提出替代品，话题自然进入 Topic B。

### Topic B — Choosing an Alternative

7. **Chris:** Maybe. What would you recommend for a stir-fry?  
   **Taylor:** These wider rice noodles should work, though the texture is a little different.
8. **Chris:** Good to know. Are they easy to cook?  
   **Taylor:** Pretty easy. Just follow the directions on the package.
9. **Chris:** Okay. Are they about the same price?  
   **Taylor:** They're a bit cheaper, actually.
10. **Chris:** Nice. Do you have a smaller package?  
    **Taylor:** Let me check. Yes, there's one right behind these.
11. **Chris:** Perfect. I'll go with that one. Thanks for your help!  
    **Taylor:** Happy to help. Anything else you're looking for?
12. **Chris:** Actually, where can I find the soy sauce?  
    **Taylor:** Same aisle, near the bottom shelf.

### 难句精讲

- **I couldn't spot them.** 我没找到它们。*Spot* 在这里是“看见、发现”，不是“斑点”。
- **Looks like we're out of stock.** 看来我们缺货了。*Looks like…* 是口语化判断，不是百分之百保证。
- **Would you be open to trying a different kind?** 你愿意试试另一种吗？*be open to + 名词/-ing* 表示愿意考虑。
- **I'll go with that one.** 我就选那个吧。*go with* 在这里表示做出选择。

**注意：** *aisle* 中的 **s 不发音**。*out of stock* 适用于超市、网购、药店等很多场景。

## ③ Reusable Patterns｜三个高频句型

**A. Could you help me find …?** 礼貌询问商品位置。  
*Could you help me find the olive oil?* 能帮我找一下橄榄油吗？

**B. Do you have … instead?** 询问替代选择。  
*Do you have a smaller size instead?* 有没有小一号的？

**C. I'll go with …** 表示做出决定。  
*I'll go with the cheaper option.* 我选便宜一点的那个。

## ④ Pronunciation｜两个自然发音技巧

**1. Aisle /aɪl/**：只读一个音节，和 *I'll* 同音。*Which aisle?* /wɪtʃ aɪl/，不要读出 /s/。

**2. Out of → /ˈaʊɾə/**：在自然美式语流中，*out of stock* 的 *out of* 常连读，中间 /t/ 可能变成闪音 [ɾ]。先读清楚 /aʊt əv/，再逐渐加快。

**英美差别：** 美式英语中超市常说 *grocery store*；英式英语常说 *supermarket*。两个词在两边都能理解，且 *grocery store* 不一定是大型超市。

## ⑤ Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

**Taylor:** We might get a delivery Thursday, but I can't guarantee it.

**Chris:** Oh, I see. So it might not be back in stock before the weekend. Is there something similar I could use tonight?

- **React:** *Oh, I see.* — 先回应。
- **Pick up a detail:** *It might not be back in stock before the weekend.* — 抓住“补货时间不确定”。
- **Ask a follow-up:** *Is there something similar I could use tonight?* — 顺着“今晚要用”的需求继续追问。

如果店员说 *These are a little wider*，你可以接：*Got it. Would that make a big difference when cooking?* 这比突然换一个问题更自然。

## ⑦ Speaking Challenge｜连续三轮角色扮演

**Round 1 — Find an item**  
**Taylor:** *Hi! Can I help you find something?*  
请说你想买什么、已经找过哪个区域，然后反问它通常放在哪个货架。

**Round 2 — React to a shortage**  
**Taylor:** *Sorry, we're out of stock. We might get more later this week.*  
先回应，再抓住补货时间不确定的细节，问是否有今晚能用的替代品。

**Round 3 — Compare and decide**  
**Taylor:** *We have two alternatives. One is cheaper, and the other is closer to what you wanted.*  
询问包装大小或使用区别，选一个，并解释为什么。最后用 *Anything else you'd recommend?* 延续聊天。
