# Day 27 — Clarifying a Vague Task → Agreeing on Priorities and a Deadline

**今日话题：** 听到一个不够明确的工作任务 → 追问具体要求，并在发现截止时间变化后重新确认优先级。

**Today's goal:** Ask what a task actually involves, check that you understood it, and agree on a realistic order and deadline without sounding difficult.

## Real Conversation｜12 轮原创对话

*Situation: Sam and Riley are teammates preparing a short project demo. Riley has asked Sam to “clean up the results,” but the request is vague.*

### Topic A — Clarifying a Vague Task

1. **Riley:** Hey, do you have a minute? I saw your message about the demo.  
   **Sam:** Yeah, what's up?

2. **Sam:** You asked me to “clean up the results.” What exactly do you mean by that?  
   **Riley:** The charts are a little hard to read. Could you make them clearer?

3. **Sam:** Got it. Are you looking for a new layout, or do you want me to check the numbers too?  
   **Riley:** Mainly the layout, but please flag anything that looks off.

4. **Sam:** Okay. Should I keep the same metrics?  
   **Riley:** Yeah, keep those. Just make the labels and units easier to understand.

5. **Sam:** That helps. Is there an example you want me to follow?  
   **Riley:** There's a slide from last week's update. You can use that as a reference.

6. **Sam:** Perfect. How polished does it need to be for today's check-in?  
   **Riley:** Actually, that's the thing — we've moved the check-in to noon.

### Natural Transition｜为什么转到 Topic B？

Sam 原本以为自己只需要把图表整理得更清楚。追问之后，他又发现团队把 check-in 提前到了中午。新的时间要求改变了任务的紧迫程度，也让 Sam 必须考虑手头的其他工作。

所以话题自然从“任务具体要做什么”转向“先做哪件事、哪些内容可以稍后完成”。这不是换了一个无关场景，而是同一项工作的下一步沟通。

### Topic B — Agreeing on Priorities and a Deadline

7. **Sam:** Oh, I didn't catch that. I thought it was at the end of the day.  
   **Riley:** Yeah, the team wants to review the slides before lunch.

8. **Sam:** I can get a first pass done by eleven, but I may not have time to check every chart.  
   **Riley:** That's fine. The main thing is to make the three key charts readable.

9. **Sam:** So the priority is those three charts, and the rest can wait?  
   **Riley:** Exactly. If you have time, add a short note about what changed.

10. **Sam:** I also have the test results to send to QA this morning. Which one should I tackle first?  
    **Riley:** Let's do the charts first. The QA update can wait until one.

11. **Sam:** Okay, I'll send you a draft by eleven and the QA results by one. Does that work?  
    **Riley:** Yep. If anything slips, just give me a heads-up.

12. **Sam:** Will do. I'll message you if I run into anything.  
    **Riley:** Perfect. Thanks for checking instead of guessing.

### 四个重要难句

- **What exactly do you mean by “clean up the results”?**  
  你说的“整理一下结果”具体是指什么？这里的 *what exactly do you mean by ...?* 是礼貌澄清模糊要求的好方法。

- **Please flag anything that looks off.**  
  如果发现任何看起来不对劲的地方，请标出来。这里的 *flag* 是“指出、标记需要注意的问题”，不只是“旗帜”。

- **I can get a first pass done by eleven, but I may not have time to check every chart.**  
  我可以在十一点前完成第一版，但可能没时间检查每一张图。这个句型既说明能完成什么，也提前讲清限制。

- **If anything slips, just give me a heads-up.**  
  如果进度有变化或可能延误，提前跟我说一声。*slip* 在这里表示计划或时间往后推。

## Reusable Patterns｜三个可复用句型

### 1. What exactly do you mean by ...?

用于澄清模糊的任务、要求或说法。

- What exactly do you mean by “make it better”?
- What exactly do you need from me?
- What exactly should I include in the update?

### 2. Just to make sure we're on the same page, ...?

用于确认双方理解一致，不显得咄咄逼人。

- Just to make sure we're on the same page, you need the charts by noon, right?
- Just to make sure we're on the same page, I'm only changing the layout, not the data.

### 3. I can do X by Y, but I may need to move Z to later.

用于现实地协商工作量和截止时间。

- I can finish the slides by noon, but I may need to move the report to tomorrow.
- I can check the main cases today, but I may need to leave the edge cases for later.

## Pronunciation｜两个自然美式发音技巧

### 1. What do you ...?

在自然美式语流中，*do you* 常弱化并连成接近 /dʒə/ 的声音；前面的 /t/ 如果处在合适的语流环境中，也可能变成轻闪音。

- 慢速：What / do / you / mean?
- 自然语流：What d'you mean?（接近 /wʌɾʒə ˈmin/）

练习：**What do you mean by “clean up”?**

重点是把整句话连起来，不要每个词都用同样的力度。

### 2. 连读：by eleven

*by* 结尾的 /aɪ/ 和 *eleven* 开头的元音自然衔接，中间可以出现轻微的 /j/ 过渡感。

练习：

- by eleven
- send it by eleven
- I'll send you a draft by eleven.

把重音放在 **ELEVEN**，不要把每个小词都读得很重。

## Conversation Skills｜React → Pick up a detail → Ask a follow-up

同事说：

**“The team wants to review the slides before lunch.”**

不要只说 *Okay.*，试着把对话推进一步：

1. **React:** “Oh, I see.”  
   哦，我明白了。

2. **Pick up a detail:** “So the review is at noon, not at the end of the day.”  
   所以评审是在中午，而不是今天下班前。

3. **Ask a follow-up:** “Which parts do you want me to focus on first?”  
   你希望我先重点处理哪些部分？

合起来：

**“Oh, I see. So the review is at noon, not at the end of the day. Which parts do you want me to focus on first?”**

如果对方说 *The three key charts are the most important*, 你可以继续：

**“Got it. Is there one chart the team is especially concerned about?”**

这样你不是机械地问完一个问题就结束，而是抓住对方给出的细节继续追问。

## Speaking Challenge｜三个连续角色扮演问题

*Scenario: You're talking to a teammate about a task for a project demo. Keep the conversation going instead of giving one-line answers.*

### Round 1 — Clarify the request

**Teammate:** “Could you clean up the results before the meeting?”

用英语问清楚：

- 对方希望你具体修改什么？
- 是否需要检查数据，还是只调整展示方式？
- 有没有可以参考的例子？

### Round 2 — React to a changed deadline

**Teammate:** “Actually, the meeting has been moved to noon.”

先回应，再抓住时间变化追问：

- 你原本以为几点开会？
- 现在能完成哪一部分？
- 哪些内容可能需要稍后处理？

### Round 3 — Agree on priorities

**Teammate:** “The three main charts matter most. The rest can wait.”

说明你手头还有另一项任务，然后协商先后顺序。最后用一句话复述双方达成的安排，并问：

**“Does that work for you?”**

## Today's Takeaway｜今日重点复习

1. **What exactly do you mean by ...?** — 你说的……具体是什么意思？
2. **Just to make sure we're on the same page ...** — 我确认一下我们理解一致……
3. **I'll get a first pass done by eleven.** — 我会在十一点前完成第一版。
4. **Which one should I tackle first?** — 我应该先处理哪一项？
5. **Give me a heads-up if anything changes.** — 如果有变化，提前告诉我一声。
